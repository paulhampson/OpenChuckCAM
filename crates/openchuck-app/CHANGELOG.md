# Changelog

All notable changes to `openchuck-app` are recorded here.

## Unreleased

- Version 0.10.0 with the library family (`assemble::smoothed_contacts` in `openchuck-path` takes the rotary mapping, a breaking change; decision 0115).
- Decision 0111 (lane Q2, OI-238): swarf on a STEP part requires a `Faces` set, which chooses the walls it cuts (as it chooses the holes for radial drilling); without one, or with an empty one, it is refused with swarf's own message, and the form says so; the faces are checked before swarf runs (ruled within the tolerance, rulings not along the rotary axis), every face it cannot cut named; swarf on a mesh part is refused as B-rep only, naming the part, in the operation's parameter form (its first note) and when generated.
- OI-259: the inspection of a STEP model checks its tessellation with the faces' shared corners joined, so a closed part is reported closed with its volume; the pole slivers joining collapses are left out, and the report's indices are the placed mesh's.
- Slice 11b wiring (lane O, decision 0066, OI-242): the scene reads a STEP part with `model::placed_part` and draws it with its own edges through `part_mesh`; `save_selection` and set edits check face and edge sets against the placed B-rep; `operation::edge_options` keeps tangent edges when one of the operation's `Edges` sets resolves to keep (`Project::keeps_tangent_edges`); edge following follows a mesh's chosen edges beside lane Z's B-rep edges.
- Slice 11b (lane O, decision 0066): `scene::SceneBrep`, `SceneEdge` and `SceneMesh::brep` (a STEP part's face per triangle and its edges); `pick::EdgeRef`, `SceneEdgePick`, `ScenePick::face`, `ScenePicker::pick_edge`, `select_faces`, `select_edges`, `edge_points` and `edges_points`, with `PickTolerance`, `FaceId` and `EdgeId` re-exported; `save_selection` takes any target and `SelectionEdit::Add`, `Remove` and `Replace` a target of the set's kind; `SelectionEdit::KeepTangentEdges`, `Command::SetKeepTangentEdges` and `Command::SetWarnOnSelectionReset`; edge following follows a mesh's chosen edges.
- Decision 0056 (lane Z): STEP models can be added, inspected, drawn and simulated (their joined tessellation), and operations on a STEP part run on its exact `BrepSurface`. Radial drilling drills the part's holes, or those of the selected `Faces` sets; edge following follows the B-rep's own edges, or the selected `Edges` sets, with the operation's crease angle (the B-rep default of 1 degree when unset, which the form shows) and shortest chain. Other selections on a STEP part are refused by name, and so is an empty face or edge set, with a message saying to select something or take the set out (it is not read as the whole part). Triangle selection refuses a STEP model.
- Decision 0057: `operation::EdgeOptions`, `edge_options` (answers off until lane O stores the per-selection setting) and `generate_operation_with`; keeping tangent edges builds the B-rep edges at a crease angle of zero, and is refused on a mesh part.
- Version 0.9.0 with the library family (decision 0076): Makera Z1 jobs are timed so the Z1's F word stays within its limits where A counts in it, and the posts write small feeds to three significant figures.
- Slice 15 (decision 0044): `rest_machining` and `pencil_finishing` are offered; an operation whose strategy cleans up earlier operations' material (`Strategy::needs_earlier_operations`) says in its notes when no earlier operation of its setup has cut.
- `JobHandle::try_result` remembers that it has handed the result over, so a poll right after it returns the documented lost-job error rather than `None`: the worker drops its end of the channel only after sending, and a poll in between read as still running (the race behind `req_nfr_04_scene_job_can_be_polled`).
- Version 0.7.0 with the library family. `rotary_roughing` is offered; the tool's stepdown fills the operation's; a plunge is refused for a tool that cannot cut straight down; `SetupSimulation::id`; `path` re-export (decision 0033).
- Slice 10 (decision 0032): `selection` module: `Application::select_region`, `save_selection`, `edit_selection`, `RegionRequest`, `SelectionEdit`; operations limited to selection sets, with unresolved references refused by name (UI-04, UI-05, PRJ-06, OP-02).
- `Application::start_select_region`: region selection as a background job (UI-09); `select_region` waits for it.
- `Application::start_prepare_model` and `add_prepared_model` (with `model::PreparedModel`): a model is read, hashed and checked as a job and added as one undoable step, so a front end never waits on a large file; `add_model_with` uses them.
- The scene's "A+" label sits an eighth of a turn along the arrow, clear of the work Y label (owner's visual check, decision 0029).
- Slice 8, libraries and machines (decision 0029 items 15 and 16): the `library` module (default machine and tool library folders in the platform configuration folder); `Command::SetMachineFrom`; `setup::available_machines`, `compare_machine` and `MachineSource`; the profile entry functions and `new_machine_id` re-exported.
- Slice 8, import (decision 0029 item 13): `Application::start_import_preview` (`model::ImportPreview` with the problems as a `SceneRole::Problem` mesh and lines), `add_model_with` (unit and placement in one step) and `model_file_warnings`; `scene::Mesh` re-exported.
- Slice 8, tool editing (decision 0029 item 12): the `tool_edit` module (tool form fields, `set_tool_field`, `set_tool_profile`, `check_profile`, `starting_profile`, `profile_outline` and segment edits) and `Application::set_tool_field` and `set_tool_profile`.
- Slice 8, parameter forms (decision 0029 item 11): the `form` module (`parameter_form`, fields with kind, unit, range, value and source), `Application::parameter_form` and `set_parameter`, which refuses an invalid value with the project unchanged.
- Slice 8, tree commands (decision 0029 item 10): `Command::SetOperationEnabled` and `duplicate_operation`; regeneration (and so simulation and posting) and the tool-number plan skip disabled operations.
- Slice 8, first step (decision 0029): the `scene` module and `Application::start_scene` (the viewport's view model: part with feature edges, stock outline, rotary axis, A+ arrow from the shared kinematics, work coordinate system, labels and notes); `JobHandle::try_result`; `Application::is_modified` and `location`.
- Slice 7 (decision 0025): `Command::SetSimulation`; `Application::simulate` and `start_simulate`; `Regeneration::simulation` with a `SimulationReport`; operations after the first in a setup are generated against the simulated stock (`generate_operation_on`, SIM-06); `OperationPath` gains the tool profile and part bounds; `openchuck_sim` re-exported as `sim`. `openchuck-cli simulate`.
- Slice 9 items 1 and 2 (decision 0030): an in-memory pipeline cache with keys chained from operation to operation, so editing operation N regenerates N and later operations only; `Regeneration` gains `generated` and `reused`; `Application::cache_versions` and `set_cache_versions` set how many results are kept per operation (`DEFAULT_CACHE_VERSIONS` = 3, 0 = off); error `Cache`. Registering a strategy clears the cache.
- Feed and material (decision 0018 item 7): `parameter_values` returns `ResolvedValues` with notes where an operation's value overrides the material's cutting data; a required feed, plunge feed or stepover with no source is refused naming the operation, the material and the tool; `generate_operation` returns `OperationPath` (group and notes). `openchuck-cli` prints regeneration notes as warnings in `validate` and `regenerate`.
- Slice 5, first step (decision 0018): the `operation` module maps an operation to the pathing library (`generate_operation`, `parameter_values` with feed, plunge feed and stepover from the tool's cutting data); regeneration runs every operation in machining order and `Regeneration` gains `notes`; `Application::strategies` and `register_strategy`; errors `UnknownOperation`, `Operation` and `Path`; `Registry` and `Strategy` re-exported.
- Slice 4 (decision 0014): the `tools` module: `check_tools` (shared tool numbers, missing cutting data, library and tool set differences), `default_controller` and `NumberSource` (TL-11), `find_tool`, `describe`, `describe_controller` and `with_custom_profile`, and re-exports of the tool and library types a front end needs.
- Slice 3 (decision 0011): `Command::SetMachine` takes a machine profile; `Application::check_setups` and `find_setup`; `ModelInspection::extent`; the `setup` module re-exports what a front end needs and states the A direction in words.
- `Application::add_model`, `find_model`, `inspect_model` and `inspect_file` as jobs; commands `SetModelUnits` and `SetModelTransform`; the `model` module re-exports the model types a front end needs (slice 2, decision 0010).
- Commands with undo and redo for every project edit (slice 1, UI-08). `openchuck-project` is re-exported as `project`.
- `Application::create`, `open` (loads `project.toml`), `save` and `check_models`. A folder without a project gives `AppError::NoProject`, which a front end answers by offering `create`.
- Skeleton: commands, queries, event stream, job manager and an empty pipeline (work breakdown slice 0).
