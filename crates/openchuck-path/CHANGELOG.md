# Changelog

## 0.27.0

- **Breaking (0.27.0, BUG-SWARF4, decision 0272; issue #296):** swarf's gate samples the motion and the face finely enough that its own error is at most `swarf::GATE_RESOLUTION` (0.0005 mm) whatever the tolerance, so the overcut it reports is the written path's (it under-reported by up to 27 times on the blisk, with a false all-clear). `FacePath::overcut_warning` takes a `Clearing`: the tolerance it advises is checked by making the face again at it (`clearing_tolerance`, up to `CLEARING_TRIES` times). The path, the pose spacing and the G-code are unchanged; generation takes about 3.5 times as long on the blisk's blades.

## 0.26.0

- **Breaking (0.26.0):** `Pass::helical_pass_start` distinguishes complete roughing-layer helices from fragments. Host struct literals use `false` for ordinary cuts/fragments, marking only the first retained fragment of a complete helix as `true`. Assembly marks existing clear transitions without changing any physical segment; connections preserve/remap indices. Existing limited-axis fitting/refusal is unchanged (accepted 0247, #337). Library family advances together to 0.26.0 under decision 0048.

## 0.23.0

- STEPALL-M release integration: optional host stage timing (types/path) and corrected XYZ rounding at the written rotary angle (post/replay, issue #324); retain main’s GC-17 block-time and rotary-boundary APIs. Library family advances together after 0.22.0.

## 0.22.0

- Lockstep library-family version for GC-17’s machine-profile and post API additions (accepted decision 0222, lane GATES). Path geometry and G-code replay behavior are unchanged.


All notable changes to `openchuck-path` are recorded here.

## Unreleased

- CARVEPERF (issue #382, decision 0292): fine finishing with a V-bit is faster and its output is unchanged, bit for bit. The mesh drop-cutter skips an edge that cannot beat the contact found so far (its higher end less the profile at its nearest approach to the axis); `CompositeSurface` skips a solid that lies wholly below the part's contact, and a box solid out of the tool's reach answers at once; a region's candidate intervals look only at the triangles near the pass in `u` and skip a triangle whose interval is already found. Internal only: no public API change, so the library family's version is not bumped. New example `carving_profile` (timings of a strategy on the carving test piece).

- STEPALL-X2 (issue #349, decision 0277): exact ball drops on STEP parts (OI-81). `BrepSurface::drop_ball` drops a tool whose lower envelope is a ball (a ball end, or one grown for stock to leave) onto the exact faces by a branch and bound: every evaluated face point is a real contact, every part passed over has a proven upper bound within `DROP_TOLERANCE` (1e-4 mm) of the best found; analytic interiors by their offset surfaces in closed form, B-spline interiors by the hull cells of decision 0260 with a new normal-cone (critical-point) test that passes over cells where the ball cannot rest tangentially (polynomial patches; not along a crease), boundaries by the segment bounds of decision 0260. `BrepSurface::with_exact_drops(true)` makes `SurfaceSource::drop_tool` use it for a ball (the tessellation for any other tool). Provided methods `SurfaceSource::drop_accuracy` (0 for a mesh) and `SurfaceSource::within` (a limited search on exact faces), and `Strategy::exact_contact` (default false; true for helical, indexed, rest and simultaneous finishing). `RegionConstraint::allows` and the watched-surface warnings accept differences within the drops' accuracy; meshes are unchanged. Additive API: `ExactDrop`, `ball_radius`, `DROP_TOLERANCE`.
- HELICALFACES (issue #384, decision 0297, proposed): helical finishing, continuous rotary roughing and the simultaneous spiral turn once round the axis at constant X at each face across the rotary axis, where the side of the tool touches it, so such faces keep only the stock to leave instead of a step up to one pitch thick (up to 2.3 mm on the owner's CrankShaft before). Paths and G-code change for parts with such faces when they are regenerated; parts with none are unchanged to the byte. The operation's notes name the faces. Facing (Round) and the indexed and axial forms are unchanged. The helix of helical finishing and the spiral of simultaneous finishing are unchanged; their turns are separate passes after them, linked by the shared services. Roughing's turns are in each layer's helix. Cost on the CrankShaft: +34 % program time. The blisk and gate 5 first-cut golden programs are re-blessed: the first-cut program the owner physically ran on 24 Oct 2026 (decisions 0221 and 0244) is no longer what that project generates; it stays at commit `0620cd0`. No public API change.

- STEPALL-X1 (issue #348, decision 0260): exact face and trim queries. `ExactFace::closest` no longer answers a point on a face's own boundary on the wrong edge (OI-839: a seam face's rim was one segment whose ends meet, and the sample search bracketed the wrong end). Trim loops are indexed (strips in v for the trim test; per-segment bounds from first- and second-derivative bounds for the boundary search), B-spline faces search a tree of Bezier hull cells (branch and bound to `brep::SEARCH_TOLERANCE`, 1e-6 mm), and faces are visited from a boundary-point upper bound. Additive API: `ExactFace::closest_within` / `closest_from`, `BrepSurface::project_within` / `project_from` / `faces_near` / `faces_in_reach`. Answers are never further than before on the corpus and blisk benchmark; tool drops still use the tessellation (OI-81).
