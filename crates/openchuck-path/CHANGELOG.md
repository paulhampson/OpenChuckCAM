# Changelog

All notable changes to `openchuck-path` are recorded here.

## Unreleased

- Version 0.17.0 with the library family (`Tab` in `openchuck-types` gains `auto`, and `RotaryFrame::machine` is removed, breaking changes; decisions 0201 and 0202).
- Version 0.16.0 with the library family (`Strategy` in `openchuck-path` gains the required method `description` and the provided `group`, a breaking change; decision 0204).
- **Breaking (0.16.0):** every strategy supplies a plain-language `Strategy::description` (a sentence or two a reader who is not a machinist can follow; issues 195 and 242; OP-16) and may override `Strategy::group` (a new `StrategyGroup`: `Roughing`, `Finishing` the default, `Specialist`). `Registry::in_menu_order` lists the strategies roughing first, then finishing, then the specialist ones (edge following, swarf, radial drilling), each group in registration order; `Registry::iter` is unchanged. A host that implements `Strategy` adds `description`. Also (issue 199): an entry by ramp or lead on a pass that is a single cut point, which has no line to follow, is made by a plunge instead of being refused, and a lead-out on it is left out; the message "the ramp needs a direction to follow" is gone.
- Version 0.15.0 with the library family (`PostJob` and `ConvertOptions` in `openchuck-post` gain `operation_spindle`, a breaking change; decision 0196).
- Version 0.14.0 with the library family (`RegionConstraint` in `openchuck-path` gains `boundary_limits` and `watch`, a breaking change; decision 0176).
- **Breaking (0.14.0):** swarf honours boundary, avoid and check surfaces (decision 0176, issue #227; OP-02). `Swarf` now honours a region (`honours_region`) and takes a `RegionConstraint` through `generate_in_region`: its boundary limits the pass across the rulings and the cut length at the tool tip, as the constraint's new `boundary_limits` say (a new `BoundaryLimits`, `pass` and `tip`, both true by default; both off makes the path without the boundary, with a warning), and a tool position too close to an avoid or check surface is left out, so the pass stops or splits there; a selected region is refused. `RegionConstraint` has two new public fields, `boundary_limits` and `watch`: surfaces only measured (a new `WatchedSurface`, with a name and a new `Protection`, avoid or check), along which swarf keeps the path and warns, naming the surface. `Strategy::honours_restriction_options` (provided, default false; swarf true) and `RegionConstraint::has_restriction_options` are new, and the shared generation refuses a region that sets either for a strategy that does not honour them. `swarf::face_path` takes the constraint; `swarf::FacePath` has a new field, `limits`, a new `swarf::Limits` saying what each limit left uncut and which watched surfaces the tool breaks (a new `swarf::WatchedBreak`); `Limits` is not `Copy`. A face the limits leave nothing of is refused, naming it. Code that builds a `RegionConstraint` field by field adds `..RegionConstraint::default()`.
- Version 0.13.0 with the library family (`StrategyOutput` and `GeneratedPath` in `openchuck-path` gain `warnings`, a breaking change; decision 0175).
- **Breaking (0.13.0):** a toolpath can be made with warnings (decision 0175, issue #226; NFR-12). New `PathWarning` (`message`, and `parameter`: the operation parameter that, changed, would accept or remove it). `StrategyOutput` and `GeneratedPath` have a new public field, `warnings: Vec<PathWarning>`; the shared generation copies a strategy's warnings to the path and adds each to the path's notes as well, first, so a host that shows only notes still says it. A strategy or a host that builds either struct adds `warnings: Vec::new()`. Swarf no longer refuses a face it would cut into by more than the tolerance: the path is made, with a warning that names the face, the depth and the place, the twist and the tilt, and says the operation's Tolerance can be raised to accept the cut (`parameter` is `tolerance`); the default tolerance (0.01 mm) is unchanged, and material left beyond `max_material_left` is still refused. `swarf::FacePath::refusal` takes only `max_material_left` (its `tolerance` argument went with the gouge refusal) and the new `FacePath::overcut_warning` gives the warning.
- `Strategy::needs_brep_part` (provided, default `None`): why a strategy needs a B-rep (STEP) part, for a host to disable it on a mesh part before an operation is added (decision 0174, issue #206). Swarf and radial drilling give their reason; the other built-in strategies work on a mesh.
- Version 0.12.0 with the library family (`StrategyInput` in `openchuck-path` gains `keep_out`, a breaking change; decision 0161).
- **Breaking (0.12.0):** `StrategyInput` has a new public field, `keep_out: &[(f64, f64)]`: model X intervals the tool's tip never enters while it cuts, each held region widened by the tool's largest radius (SET-09; decision 0161, issue #202). The shared generation cuts every strategy's passes short where they would enter one (a drilled hole is kept or dropped whole), says so in the path's notes, refuses an operation with nothing left, and refuses an assembled move at feed that still reaches in. A host with no held region passes `&[]`; its paths are unchanged. Rotary roughing limits its range to the free length between keep-out intervals and, where one cuts it short, turns its continuous helix once at that end without advancing, so the stock beside a held region is cleared all round. The notes and refusals name the held region by its end of the stock, and an entry or exit (lead, ramp, helix) that would cut into a held region is refused with a message that names it and suggests a plunge entry.
- `Solid::cuboid` and `Solid::as_cuboid` (decision 0160, issues #187 and #188): a box with faces parallel to the model's planes, the held region of box stock, with exact closest point, ray and tool drop (the drop by the mesh drop-cutter's tests on its twelve triangles). New functions only; `Solid` keeps its private fields and stays `Copy`.
- Ready to publish to crates.io (decision 0138): an `include` list (the library sources and the files they read; the tests stay out of the package), keywords and categories, and a README that links the repository. No API change.
- Version 0.11.0 with the library family (`PostJob` in `openchuck-post` gains `generator`, a breaking change; decision 0151).
- Simultaneous finishing's own path fitter tests the quarter points of a move as well as its middle (decision 0133, OI-348; OP-12, NFR-05), as the shared fitter has since decision 0115: a move is halved unless the straight machine move is within the chord limit at its middle and at the middles of its halves, each drop handed down to the half it belongs to. Before, a move whose middle lay on the chord was kept whatever happened along it, and on the exact cubic ramp the tool cut 0.19 mm into the surface a quarter of the way along a move. The lobed reference job's moves and golden programs are unchanged; generation takes about twice as long on it (0.2 s to 0.5 s). No public item changed.
- **Breaking (0.10.0):** `assemble::smoothed_contacts` takes the `RotaryMapping` the pass is assembled with (decision 0115 item 5, OI-331; OP-13): the smoothed contact is now the point's own tip plus the mean of its window's contact offsets in the machine's fixed frame, not the mean of the contact positions, so a point path fitting adds between two seeds no longer shifts its neighbours' contacts and the short moves beside it keep their contact feed (the groove reference job's went a third slow). A host passes the mapping it assembles with. Every golden program's F words fall by about 0.73 % (the old mean lay inside the path by the window's sagitta, so the contact lengths were short and the programs ran that much fast at the contact).
- Path fitting tests the quarter points of a move as well as its middle (decision 0115, OI-331; OP-12, NFR-05): the shared fitter of helical, axial and rest machining halves a move unless the straight machine move is within the chord limit at its middle and at the middles of its halves, handing each drop down to the half it belongs to; a quarter a region constraint forbids halves the move so the half finds the gap. Before, a move whose middle lay on the chord was kept whatever happened along it, and the blisk's helical pass cut 0.2 mm into its blades. The blisk, cone and groove golden programs change where such moves were split. No public item changed.
- Slice 11b (lane O, decision 0066): `BrepEdges::edge_turns`, the kept edges with the sharpest turn between their faces, for the viewport's edge drawing and face selection by angle. New item only.
- Slice 11b (lane O, decision 0066; OI-204): `curves::ChosenSegments`, a `CurveSource` over edges chosen one by one as segments (a mesh's chosen triangle edges), chained at corners where exactly two meet. New type only.
- Slice 17, lane Q (decision 0061, accepted; OP-07): `swarf` with `Swarf` (`swarf`: flank milling along the rulings of exact faces, with `detect_rulings`, `Detection`, `Family`, `FamilyFit`, `Flank`, `Settings`, `Pose`, `FacePath`, `face_path` and `DEFAULT_MAX_MATERIAL_LEFT`; a gouge beyond the tolerance and material left beyond `max_material_left` refuse the face; zero takes 0.1 mm on every pass, `Settings::material_left_limit`), `surface::FaceRef`, and `SurfaceSource::exact_faces` (provided, empty for a mesh; `BrepSurface` and `CompositeSurface` answer it); `Registry::builtin` holds the strategy. New types and functions and one provided trait method only; no field was added to a public struct.
- Version 0.9.0 with the library family (decision 0076). Path assembly times each fed move so that, on a machine whose `MaxFeeds::rotary_in_feed` is `Makera`, the length the controller counts for A, `dA * (2 pi r + 30) / 360` with the written rounding allowed for, also stays within the line's limit; before, the Makera Z1's F word went to 1432 against 1200 where A turned at its limit round a square part's corner (OI-236). Unchanged on a `Separate` machine.
- Slice 16, lane P (decision 0053, accepted; OP-08): `brep_edges` with `BrepEdges`, a `CurveSource` over the edges of a `BrepModel` (seams never, tangent edges dropped below a crease angle of 1 degree by default, boundary and non-manifold edges always; chains joined by corner in a deterministic order; `with_crease`, `with_min_length`, `with_edges`, `edge_ids`). New types and functions only; no field was added to a public struct.
- Walls in the target (decision 0050, OI-209; OP-13, OP-14): the shared services merge the bisection cascade of micro-moves beside a move that climbs or drops a wall (within the path-fitting chord limit), stop the contact smoothing at such a move, and time a move that changes X or Y by less than 0.001 mm against the lower of the linear and Z limits. Paths without a wall, and passes with rapid points (drilling), are unchanged. No public item changed.
- Slice 18 (decision 0051; OP-10): `holes` with `Hole`, `HoleEnd` and `find_holes` (round holes found from the cylindrical faces of a B-rep, with their ends read from the neighbouring faces), `drill` with `RadialDrilling` (`radial_drilling`: one pass per hole at its A, equal pecks as expanded moves, one sweep of the angles), `SurfaceSource::holes` (provided, empty for a mesh; `BrepSurface` and `CompositeSurface` answer it), and `Registry::builtin` holds the strategy.
- **Breaking (0.8.0):** `Pass` has a new public field, `rapids: Vec<usize>` (the points reached by a rapid move, for the retracts of a peck); a pass with any keeps its contact points unsmoothed.
- Slice 16, lane K (decision 0047, accepted; OP-08): `curves` with the `CurveSource` trait, `EdgeChain`, `MeshFeatureEdges` and `feature_chains`, and `edge_following` with `EdgeFollowing` (`edge_following`: engrave and carve along edge chains with the tool axis radial, constant radial depth, V-bit line width following depth, levels, start and direction controls) and `follow_chains`; `Registry::builtin` holds it. New types and functions only; no field was added to a public struct.
- Slice 15 (decision 0044): `rest` with `RestMachining` (`rest_machining`) and `RemainingMap` (remaining material found through `StockModel` by probing the stock near samples of the target), `pencil` with `PencilFinishing` (`pencil_finishing`), `Crease` and `concave_creases`; `Strategy::needs_earlier_operations` (provided, default false); `Registry::builtin` holds both. No field was added to a public struct.
- Slice 21b (decision 0042; SET-09, OP-21): `Solid` (a capped cylinder or frustum on any axis with exact volume, area, bounds, closest point and normal, ray, and a tool drop that maximises `z - h(r)` over the ruled boundary) and `CompositeSurface` (a `SurfaceSource` over a model's source and a list of solids: the highest contact for a drop, the nearest point, the first ray hit). New types and functions only; no field was added to a public struct and the port is unchanged.
- Slice 13 (decision 0036): `simultaneous` with `SimultaneousFinishing` (`simultaneous_finishing`: spiral and axial drive patterns, the tool axis on the smoothed surface normal within a lead angle and a largest tilt, A smoothed by the taut string with the fewest reversals, the normal-offset fast path seeding the drop for convex tools, a gouge check at the middle of every machine move) and `taut_string`; `Registry::builtin` holds it; `Strategy::passes_with_notes` (provided) and `StrategyOutput`, whose notes `generate` adds to the path's notes. No field was added to a public struct.
- Version 0.7.0 with the library family (`Tool` gains `centre_cutting` and `Pass` gains `stock`, breaking changes; decision 0033).
- **Breaking (0.7.0):** `Pass` has a new public field, `stock: Option<PassStock>` (the material when the pass starts). New: `RotaryRoughing` (`rotary_roughing`, OP-03), `HeightStock` (`field`), `EntryParameters::check_tool`, and local links and width-wide entry probes for passes that carry a stock (decision 0033).
- Version 0.6.0 with the library family (`PostJob` and `ConvertOptions` in `openchuck-post` gain `operation_names`, a breaking change; decision 0028).
- Slice 10 (decision 0032): `RegionConstraint` and `Window` (OP-02); `Strategy::honours_region` and `Strategy::passes_in_region` (defaults refuse) and `generate_in_region`; helical finishing honours regions. No field was added to a public struct.

- Version 0.5.0 with the library family (the B-rep face record in `openchuck-types` is a breaking change, OI-123).

- Slice 11, item 6 (IMP-02, decision 0017): `brep` with `ExactFace` (a `ParametricFace` from a `BrepFace`: closed-form plane, cylinder, cone, sphere and torus, and a clamped rational B-spline evaluator with a damped closest-point solver that survives singular poles, OI-72) and `BrepSurface` (a `SurfaceSource` over the faces of a `BrepModel` or a selection of them; tool drops fall back to the tessellation, OI-81 stays open).
- Entry moves (decision 0026): `assemble::assemble_with_entry`, `EntryStyle` and `EntryParameters` (plunge, lead, ramp and helix; `EntryParameters::PLUNGE`, `EntryParameters::read`), and the common parameters `entry`, `ramp_angle`, `helix_diameter` and `lead_radius`. `generate` uses them; `assemble` is unchanged and plunges.
- Slice 5, second part (decision 0018): `helical` (helical finishing, `HelicalFinishing`, `CHORD_FACTOR` 0.4) and `Registry::builtin` holding it; `cutter::grow_profile` and `cutter::cutter_contact` for stock to leave; a pruned drop-cutter (`MeshSurface` finds the highest point on the axis line and walks the bounding volume hierarchy with a height bound); `unroll` (regions on the development, `Region::validate`, `ValidRegion::offset` and `passes`, `chord_points`, `PeriodicBand`); the port gains `tessellation`, `position: Option<FacePosition>` on `SurfacePoint` and `RayHit`, and the `ParametricFace` trait; `PathError::Region`. New dependency `cavalier_contours`.
- Slice 5, first step (decision 0018): the `SurfaceSource`, `StockModel` and `CollisionOracle` ports with `MeshSurface`, `AnalyticStock` and `AcceptAll`; the drop-cutter (`cutter`); `RotaryMapping` and the A-axis functions (`rotary`); parameter schemas and the common parameters (`params`); `Strategy`, `Registry` and `generate` (`strategy`); the shared services (`assemble`); deterministic parallel maps (`parallel`); `PathError`; the `host` example. New dependency `parry3d-f64` (bounding volume hierarchy only).
- Crate created as a shell (work breakdown slice 0).
