# Changelog

## 0.55.0 (provisional, as the Merge Manager sequences it; MSGREVIEW, decision 0644, proposed; decision 0536 step 6; issue #567)

- **Breaking, changed output (0.55.0, or the next free version):** `PathError::Refused { topic, text, setting }`, a refusal by a named check with the operation setting to change, built with `PathError::refused(topic, text)` and `.with_setting(key)`; it displays as "<topic>: <text>". New topics in `openchuck_path::topic`: End facing, Facing completion, Facing motion, Box facing, Facing check, Facing extent, Surface, Region, Lead entry, Helix entry, Setting, Stock to leave, Stock, Toolpath, Facing, Rotary roughing, Axial finishing, Helical finishing, Simultaneous 4-axis finishing and Radial drilling.
- **Changed:** every strategy refusal is a `PathError::Refused` with its check's topic, and with the setting where its text names one; the words of each text are kept ([the list](../../docs/archive/0644-refusals-take-the-one-form/after.md)). "Facing final validation: …" refusals read "Facing check: …". A connection refusal reads "Links: cannot connect safely: …", without the operation's name, and `connect_group`'s private checks no longer take the group. A host that matched `PathError::Strategy` for these refusals matches `PathError::Refused` (`PathError` is `#[non_exhaustive]`). `PathError::Strategy` remains for the registry's errors, and `Surface`, `Region` and `RotaryLimit` for the geometry's own.

## 0.54.0

- **Breaking, changed output (0.54.0, or the next free version, as the Merge Manager sequences it; RAMPENTRY; issues #639 and #642; decision 0543, accepted, the owner's answers of 8 Nov 2026 relayed; decision 0544 item 6):** every ramp entry follows the pass's own toolpath.
  - **Output.** The program of every operation with Entry Ramp changes. A ramp starts the approach distance above the highest stock under the stretch of the pass it uses and descends at no more than the ramp angle to the first cut point, where the pass is cut unchanged:
    - a line, or an arc shorter than a whole turn: back and forth over the start of the pass, an even number of legs, the last ending at the first cut point (minimum the tool radius; 10,000 legs at most);
    - a ring: round the ring before its first point, A turning the pass's way, within the angle at the ring's smallest tip radius, at most 10 turns, the extra A counted against a limited A range;
    - a spiral: round the rotary axis at the spiral's start X;
    - where the pass itself falls more steeply than the angle just before its first point, the ramp follows that fall there, and a note names the passes.
    The straight ramp behind the first cut point and the back-and-forth corridor are gone, so ramps beside a held region, on a rising part and on passes that turn A generate where they were refused. Plunge, lead and helix entries are unchanged on line passes. A pass that starts above the stock is entered as a plunge would be.
  - **Output (issue #642).** Before a lead-in or a ramp moves across at the link's height to its start, the stock under the tool is checked along that whole move; where it is not clear by the approach distance the tool rises first (never above the retract height), and a note counts such entries.
  - **Output (RAMPLEAD; issue #640; decision 0544 items 1 to 5, accepted, relayed).** A lead on a pass that starts or ends by turning A (rings, the spiral, partial rings, simultaneous finishing's rings and spiral) lies in the plane of the cut at the pass's X: the tool comes down onto the cut on an arc of the lead radius (a quarter circle where the cut is level; it turns from straight down to the cut's direction, so A never turns back), turning A the pass's way and moving Z with it, and the lead-out rises from the last point the same way; the link arrives at the A where the arc starts. The arcs are fed at the cutting feed, with a contact point only on the move that meets the surface. A pass that only turns A now gets this lead instead of a plunge with no lead-out. Such a lead outside a limited A range is refused naming A range; in a pass that carries its stock, a lead beside a partial ring that would pass through stock not yet cleared is refused naming Entry. Where a lead's plunge to its arc's start goes down through stock standing above it (on a pass that turns A and, the same rule, on a line pass, which main never listed), and where a pass that starts on the rotary axis is plunged, the pass is listed in `GeneratedPath::straight_plunges` for the host's tool check; line-lead moves are unchanged. A single-point pass under Lead is still plunged, and one information note (topic `notice::topic::LEAD_ENTRY`, "Lead entry", new) now names those passes. Leads on line passes are byte-identical. So continuous roughing with Lead entry on a part that rises along X, and leads beside a held region at a part's end, generate where they were refused or reached past the part's end.
  - **API.** `EntryParameters` gains `allow_single_point_plunge` (common parameter `allow_single_point_plunge`, a flag, off by default; `params::names::ALLOW_SINGLE_POINT_PLUNGE`): off, a single-point pass under a ramp refuses the operation naming the setting; on, it is plunged, with a note naming the passes. `ramp_back_and_forth` is still read and has no effect.
  - **Steeper ramp near the axis (decision 0543 section 4, point 3, the owner's decision).** `EntryParameters` gains `steeper_near_axis` and `ramp_angle_max` (common parameters `ramp_steeper_near_axis`, a flag, off by default, and `ramp_angle_max`, degrees, 15 by default, at most 45). With it on, a ring, spiral or on-axis ramp that would need more than 10 turns at the Ramp angle uses the angle that fits 10 turns, never steeper than the maximum; a note names the passes and the steepest angle; past the maximum the pass is refused naming Maximum ramp angle. Off, output is unchanged.
  - **Plunge to remove face nubs (decision 0543 section 4, point 3, the owner's decision).** `EntryParameters` gains `plunge_face_nubs` and `face_nub_diameter` (common parameters, off and 0 by default; 0 is the tool's cutting diameter). With it on and Entry Ramp, a pass that carries its stock (roughing) whose every point lies within the nub's radius of the rotary axis, where the tool dropped onto the axis meets nothing of the part, is replaced by plunges to the axis at most a tool diameter apart along X, each followed by a whole turn of A with the tip on the axis; deeper passes over a stretch already cleared are skipped; a note (topic Face nubs) names the stretch and the plunges. When the steeper ramp near the axis is also on, the nub plunge applies first. Off, output is unchanged.
  - **API removed (decision 0543 D10, the owner's answer).** The clearance certificate of the back-and-forth corridor goes with it: the `entry_safety` module (`EntrySafety`, `RotaryPrecision`, `SweepSession`, `Blocked`, `Refused`), `StrategyInput::entry_safety` and `EntryParameters::back_and_forth`. A host drops the `entry_safety` field from its `StrategyInput` (it was `None` unless the host supported the back-and-forth ramp) and stops reading `back_and_forth`; the `ramp_back_and_forth` parameter stays in the common schema so saved operations still open, and has no effect.
  - **Hosts.** `GeneratedPath` gains `straight_plunges`, the passes a Ramp or Lead entry enters by a straight plunge into the stock (a single point, a ring on the rotary axis, a face nub); a host that knows the tool cannot cut straight down refuses a path that lists any (the application does). The descent through air at the approach distance that every entry makes is not listed. Adding the field is a breaking change for code that builds a `GeneratedPath` literal. Generated G-code remains the user's to verify before running it on a machine.

## 0.53.0 (RINGSTEP; issues #638 and #644; decisions 0545 and 0546, accepted)

- **Changed output (RINGSTEP; issue #644; decision 0546, accepted):** with **Extend past the stock**, helical finishing's spiral and ring passes carry on past the part in their own motion, the spiral at its pitch and rings at the ring spacing joined by the operation's own steps, out to the extended end, instead of a line along X at one A; the stock past the part's end is cut all round. Every helical finishing operation with Extend on changes, saved ones too (the owner's choice Q2); no golden program uses Extend. A note gives the turns and the length of path added. Axial and indexed finishing keep the run-out along X.

## 0.52.0 (PATHFEED, issues #641 and #643; decisions 0547 and 0548, accepted)

- **Breaking (0.52.0, or the next free version, as the Merge Manager sequences it):** `Pass` gains the public field `plunge_fed`, the indices of points reached at the plunge feed as an entry move (`MoveKind::LeadIn`); assembly writes them so, and leaves such a pass unmerged at walls (decision 0050) so the indices keep their points. `facing::end::EndPassPlan` gains `passes_above_area`. `topic::STOCK_ABOVE_THE_AREA` is added. A host that builds `Pass` by literal adds `plunge_fed: Vec::new()`.
- **Changed output (End facing):**
  - End reads the operation's Stepover as the slice along X and its Stepdown as the band along the tool, as a tool's cutting data defines them (`EndFacingParameters::read`); the schema labels them "Stepover (slice thickness along X)" and "Stepdown (band along the tool)". `openchuck-project` schema 28 migrates saved End operations.
  - Where there is air beside a band's edge, the band comes in and goes out along X beside the stock, the whole tool the approach distance clear, so only the sweep across meets the face, at its edge. Where there is none (a Part extent narrower than the stock), the move along X into the face at the band's edge, and back out, is kept and goes at the plunge feed as an entry move.
  - Where the stock rises above the requested area (Part or Part plus tabs), bands from the stock's top down to the area are added, each no deeper than the Stepdown, with an information note (topic "Stock above the area") counting the passes and their time at the cutting feed (the owner's answer to question 4).
  - Final validation checks the depth each End sweep cuts along the tool, from the stock's top at its view or the band above it, against the Stepdown, and refuses a deeper cut naming it.
  - The slice check allows the written-motion share of the tolerance, so a depth that divides exactly into full-size slices posts.
- **Changed output (decision 0548, accepted by the owner on 8 Nov 2026):** in rotary roughing and in Facing's Flat and Round modes, a cutting move inside a pass that goes down along the tool axis faster than the plunge feed is slowed so its tip goes down at the plunge feed: its time becomes the descent at the plunge feed and its contact feed is lowered to match. Nothing else changes: a move already that slow, a level or rising move, entries, exits and links keep their feeds, and every other strategy (finishing included) is unchanged (OI-2134). A program changes only where a roughing pass came down faster than the plunge feed; no stored golden program is a roughing job.
- **Added:** `Strategy::limits_descent_feed` (a provided method, `false` by default) says whether a strategy takes that rule; `RotaryRoughing` returns `true`, and `Facing` returns `true` for the Flat and Round shapes.

## 0.51.0 (MSGREVIEW, decision 0538 part a and decision 0536 steps 2 and 3, and MSGSTEP5, decision 0536 step 5)

- **Changed text (MSGSTEP5; decision 0536 step 5; OI-2097; NFR-12):** the Facing validation's refusals say "checked" in place of "certified" and "verified" (for example "Round has no checked full-circumference finish"). Only the texts change; the API and the paths do not.
- **Breaking (0.51.0, or the next free version, as the Merge Manager sequences it; MSGREVIEW step 3; issue #567; decision 0536 section 3, accepted):** every notice names its check. The new module `topic` (re-exported as `openchuck_path::topic`) holds one constant per topic, and every warning and every piece of information the crate makes sets `Notice::topic` from it, so a host shows it as "<Topic> (<context>): <text>". `flutes::WARNING_LEAD` is removed: the flute check's warning carries the topic `topic::FLUTE_LENGTH`, and its text no longer starts with "Flute length: ". Nine descriptions no longer repeat their topic at the start (adaptive spacing, rest machining, finishing layers, edge following, pencil clean-up, face-flow finishing, swarf's per-face description). No path or program changes.
- **Changed (decision 0538, accepted):** the warnings that may be a collision carry `Acknowledgement::PossibleCollision`, so a host asks for a tick before writing: the flute check's warning (every case of `flutes::check_path`), swarf cutting into a face next to it (`FacePath::neighbour_warnings`), a touched check set that only warns (not an avoid set), and a free end faced down to the axis with nothing holding the part on the other side (not the stock ring). The API, the paths and the texts are unchanged.
- **Changed output (MSGREVIEW step 2; issue #567; decision 0536 step 2, accepted):** the notes that describe a real problem are now warnings, with their wording unchanged: rest machining with a tool whose profile is not convex; a shoulder the tool does not turn at because of a held region; an inside corner a round-ended tool cannot finish; selected faces that lie across the rotary axis (helical and axial finishing); a chain edge following cannot carve; an edge pencil clean-up does not follow; a hole radial drilling leaves out (pointing at Axis tolerance or Diameter tolerance); what is left uncut or undrilled beside a held region; and material simultaneous finishing leaves beyond the tolerance (pointing at Tolerance). No path or program changes.
- **Changed output (MSGREVIEW step 2; the owner's answers of 8 Nov 2026, decision 0536):** two notes are split, so that only their caution is a warning. A free end faced from the setup's own stock now has a note on how it is faced and a warning of its own: "the end face at X … is faced from … mm, the top of the setup's own stock with no earlier operation in this setup: the real blank must match it". Simultaneous finishing's note on surfaces hidden from the drive no longer ends "the tool holder is not checked"; a warning of its own says "the tool holder is not checked on the passes that finish the surfaces hidden from the drive between X … and …, where the tool leans up to … degrees". No path or program changes.
- **Changed output (MSGREVIEW step 2; issue #600):** a cutting move that turns A with the contact point at the rotary axis is no longer said as "the contact feed is 0.0 mm/min". Its note says "A turns with the contact point at the rotary axis during this move", so the rotary axis's speed limit sets the time taken, and gives the feed set; notes about consecutive such moves are one line, in the Results tab and on the command line. It is not a feed-limit note, so a host does not summarise it as one. The G-code is unchanged.

## 0.50.0

- **Breaking (0.50.0, or the next free version, as the Merge Manager sequences it; MSGREVIEW step 1; issue #567; decision 0536 item 6.1, accepted):** one type, `Notice` (module `notice`, re-exported with `Level` and `Acknowledgement`), replaces `PathNote` and `PathWarning` for everything generation tells the user about a path.

  - **Output.** No path, program or golden file changes. Every notice of this release has the level the old type gave it (a strategy's notes are information, its warnings warnings) and no topic yet; decision 0536's later steps set them.
