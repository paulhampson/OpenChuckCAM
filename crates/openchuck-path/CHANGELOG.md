# Changelog

All notable changes to `openchuck-path` are recorded here.

## Unreleased

- Slice 16, lane K (decision 0047, proposed; OP-08): `curves` with the `CurveSource` trait, `EdgeChain`, `MeshFeatureEdges` and `feature_chains`, and `edge_following` with `EdgeFollowing` (`edge_following`: engrave and carve along edge chains with the tool axis radial, constant radial depth, V-bit line width following depth, levels, start and direction controls) and `follow_chains`; `Registry::builtin` holds it. New types and functions only; no field was added to a public struct.
- Slice 15 (decision 0044): `rest` with `RestMachining` (`rest_machining`) and `RemainingMap` (remaining material found through `StockModel` by probing the stock near samples of the target), `pencil` with `PencilFinishing` (`pencil_finishing`), `Crease` and `concave_creases`; `Strategy::needs_earlier_operations` (provided, default false); `Registry::builtin` holds both. No field was added to a public struct.
- Slice 21b (decision 0042; SET-09, OP-21): `Solid` (a capped cylinder or frustum on any axis with exact volume, area, bounds, closest point and normal, ray, and a tool drop that maximises `z - h(r)` over the ruled boundary) and `CompositeSurface` (a `SurfaceSource` over a model's source and a list of solids: the highest contact for a drop, the nearest point, the first ray hit). New types and functions only; no field was added to a public struct and the port is unchanged.
- Slice 13 (decision 0036): `simultaneous` with `SimultaneousFinishing` (`simultaneous_finishing`: spiral and axial drive patterns, the tool axis on the smoothed surface normal within a lead angle and a largest tilt, A smoothed by the taut string with the fewest reversals, the normal-offset fast path seeding the drop for convex tools, a gouge check at the middle of every machine move) and `taut_string`; `Registry::builtin` holds it; `Strategy::passes_with_notes` (provided) and `StrategyOutput`, whose notes `generate` adds to the path's notes. No field was added to a public struct.
- Version 0.7.0 with the library family (`Tool` gains `centre_cutting` and `Pass` gains `stock`, breaking changes; decision 0033).
- **Breaking (0.7.0):** `Pass` has a new public field, `stock: Option<PassStock>` (the material when the pass starts). New: `RotaryRoughing` (`rotary_roughing`, OP-03), `HeightStock` (`field`), `EntryParameters::check_tool`, and local links and width-wide entry probes for passes that carry a stock (decision 0033).
- Version 0.6.0 with the library family (`PostJob` and `ConvertOptions` in `openchuck-post` gain `operation_names`, a breaking change; decision 0028).
- Slice 10 (decision 0032): `RegionConstraint` and `Window` (OP-02); `Strategy::honours_region` and `Strategy::passes_in_region` (defaults refuse) and `generate_in_region`; helical finishing honours regions. No field was added to a public struct.

- Version 0.5.0 with the library family (the B-rep face record in `openchuck-types` is a breaking change, OI-123).

- Slice 11, item 6 (IMP-02, decision 0017): `brep` with `ExactFace` (a `ParametricFace` from a `BrepFace`: closed-form plane, cylinder, cone, sphere and torus, and a clamped rational B-spline evaluator with a damped closest-point solver that survives singular poles, OI-72) and `BrepSurface` (a `SurfaceSource` over the faces of a `BrepModel` or a selection of them; tool drops fall back to the tessellation, OI-81 stays open).
- Entry moves (decision 0026): `assemble::assemble_with_entry`, `EntryStyle` and `EntryParameters` (plunge, lead, ramp and helix; `EntryParameters::PLUNGE`, `EntryParameters::read`), and the common parameters `entry`, `ramp_angle`, `helix_diameter` and `lead_radius`. `generate` uses them; `assemble` is unchanged and plunges.
- Slice 5, second part (decision 0018): `helical` (helical finishing, `HelicalFinishing`, `CHORD_FACTOR` 0.4) and `Registry::builtin` holding it; `cutter::grow_profile` and `cutter::cutter_contact` for stock to leave; a pruned drop-cutter (`MeshSurface` finds the highest point on the axis line and walks the bounding volume hierarchy with a height bound); `unroll` (regions on the development, `Region::validate`, `ValidRegion::offset` and `passes`, `chord_points`, `PeriodicBand`); the port gains `tessellation`, `position: Option<FacePosition>` on `SurfacePoint` and `RayHit`, and the `ParametricFace` trait; `PathError::Region`. New dependency `cavalier_contours`.
- Slice 5, first step (decision 0018): the `SurfaceSource`, `StockModel` and `CollisionOracle` ports with `MeshSurface`, `AnalyticStock` and `AcceptAll`; the drop-cutter (`cutter`); `RotaryMapping` and the A-axis functions (`rotary`); parameter schemas and the common parameters (`params`); `Strategy`, `Registry` and `generate` (`strategy`); the shared services (`assemble`); deterministic parallel maps (`parallel`); `PathError`; the `host` example. New dependency `parry3d-f64` (bounding volume hierarchy only).
- Crate created as a shell (work breakdown slice 0).
