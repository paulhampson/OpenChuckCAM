# OpenChuckCAM Individual Contributor Licence Agreement

Version 1.0, 11 October 2026.

Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Individual Contributor License Agreement, version 2.2. The changes are: the party receiving the licence is the OpenChuckCAM Maintainer instead of the Foundation; section 2 states the right to relicense, which the Apache grant already includes through sublicensing; section 9 adds the Maintainer's commitment to keep each contribution available under an open source licence; and the signing instructions fit a GitHub project.

Thank you for your interest in OpenChuckCAM. To clarify the intellectual property licence granted with Contributions from any person or entity, the Maintainer must have a signed Contributor Licence Agreement from each Contributor, showing agreement with the terms below. This agreement protects you as a Contributor as well as the Maintainer and the project's users. It does not change your rights to use your own Contributions for any other purpose.

**Why this agreement exists.** OpenChuckCAM is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The project may later need to offer its code, and in particular its reusable libraries, under other terms: for example, a licence exception or a dual licence so another open source project such as FreeCAD can use them (requirement INT-09). This agreement gives the Maintainer the rights needed to do that without asking every past contributor again. Section 9 commits that your Contributions stay available under an open source licence whatever happens.

## How to sign

Sign once, before your first pull request is merged. Add a comment to your pull request containing exactly:

> I have read the OpenChuckCAM Individual Contributor Licence Agreement, version 1.0, and I agree to its terms.

The comment, with your GitHub account name and the date, is your signature. If you contribute on behalf of an employer or other organisation that holds rights in your work, that organisation must also sign the [entity agreement](CLA-ENTITY.md), or confirm in writing that it waives those rights (section 4).
