# 0161: No cutting move reaches over the gripped length (SET-09; issue #202; lane BUG-HELD)

- **Status:** accepted (21 Oct 2026), by the project owner directly in the lane BUG-HELD session, with the lane's refinements of option 1 (the 0.5 mm clearance and roughing's turn at a held end, item 6). The owner chose option 1 on 20 Oct 2026, relayed by the coordinator session as owner decision 2 of 2 (decision 0049). The code on `claude/lane-bug-held-region` builds option 1. It refines [decision 0035](0035-workholding-and-retaining-tabs.md) item 1 and keeps it: the held region stays in the target.
- **Date:** 20 Oct 2026.
- **Open items:** new OI-552 and OI-553. OI-160 (chuck geometry from the machine profile, with COL-04) stays deferred.

## Context

SET-09 says the held region "is never machined, strategies and links keep clear of it". The verification plan's SET-09 row says roughing, finishing and links "never cut or cross the held region". Decision 0035 item 1 kept every strategy off the held region by making it part of the target, which every strategy treats as the finished part. That stops a cut *into* it. It does not stop a tool running *along* it: a finishing strategy follows the target, so on round stock it made fed moves along the held region's surface over the whole gripped length, where the chuck jaws are.

The lane measured this on round stock (a cone, held 4 mm at both ends, a 3 mm ball, issue #202). Helical finishing put 291 of its 2752 fed moves over the held regions, axial 407 of 2953, indexed 44 of 312 and simultaneous 292 of 2430. Edge following traced the held regions' rims: 776 of 1028 moves, sinking 0.2 mm at the stock's end face, because the composite's tessellation gives it the solid's edges. Nothing was removed from the simulated stock, but on a machine those moves run into the jaws. On box stock (decision 0160) the same moves also cut.

## Decision

1. **No part of the tool reaches over a gripped length while it cuts** (option 1 of 3, the owner's choice). In a 4-axis machine the tool is always across the rotary axis, so its body spans its tip's X plus and minus its largest radius. The host passes each held region's X interval, widened on both sides by the tool's largest radius (its cutting profile or its shank, whichever is wider) and by a clearance of 0.5 mm, as `StrategyInput::keep_out`. The tip never enters those intervals while it cuts. The clearance (`openchuck-app::operation::HELD_CLEARANCE`) is the lane's refinement of option 1: without it a link that ends flush with the jaw face leaves the tool's rim on the held stock's face, and the simulation's cells read that as a rapid move removing material. It showed as three such notes on the owner's `blisk_bad_roughing` project.
2. **Applied once, in the shared generation, for every strategy.** After a strategy gives its passes and before they are assembled (`openchuck-path::strategy::generate_with`), each pass is cut into the runs of points that stay clear, with the moves between them. A run of a single point from a longer pass is dropped. The shared linking joins what is left with its usual entries, links and retracts. A pass with rapid points (a drilled hole) is kept whole or dropped whole. This covers roughing, every finishing strategy, rest machining, pencil, edge following, drilling and swarf, and any strategy added later, without changing each one.
3. **Said and refused, never silent.** When anything is left out, the operation's notes say: "Nothing within one tool radius of a held region is cut: no part of the tool reaches over the gripped length of the stock." Holes not drilled are counted in a note. If nothing is left of the passes, generation is refused: "every cut of this operation would reach over a held region, where the workholding grips the stock, so nothing is left to cut". After assembly, any move at feed (a cut, an entry, an exit or a link) that still reaches into a keep-out interval refuses the whole path with a message naming its X. So such a program is never written (NFR-07, NFR-12).
4. **The held region stays in the target** (decision 0035 item 1). Roughing still clears up to it, the deviation report still counts any cut into it as overcut, and the simulated stock keeps it.
5. **Links at the clearance height are allowed over the held region**, as before. Whether the chuck reaches above the clearance cylinder is collision checking with chuck geometry (COL-04, P2; OI-160).
6. **Roughing clears beside the held region all round** (the lane's refinement of option 1). A roughing helix cut short at a keep-out loses part of its first turn, so it left a wedge of full-height stock, up to one pitch wide, beside the held region. A later finishing helix with a finer pitch then met that stock at full depth. On the owner's `blisk_1_overcut_mismatch` project, the ramp check found 20 mm of stock over the first finishing pass. So rotary roughing (`roughing.rs`, `cut_range`) limits its range to the free length between the keep-out intervals. Where a held region cuts the range short, the continuous form's helix turns once at that end without advancing, as the margin beyond the part's ends otherwise gives it. Without a held region the arithmetic is unchanged, so the G-code is too. The indexed form's lines along X need no such turn. Finishing strategies keep their partial first turn: the most they leave there is a wedge of their own stock to leave.
7. **Consequence of option 1, which the owner chose:** nothing within one tool radius (or shank radius) of a held region's inner face is cut, because that is where the jaw face is. The operation's notes say so when it happens.

Options not chosen: (2) keep the target-only rule, so the tool may run along the held region but not into it (paths through the jaws); (3) as option 1 but with the cutting radius only, not the shank, which lets the shank reach over the jaw.

## Consequences

- `StrategyInput` has a new public field, `keep_out: &[(f64, f64)]`. This is a breaking change to the library family, so the workspace version goes to 0.12.0 with changelog entries (decision 0048). A host with no held region passes `&[]`, and its G-code is unchanged. The application computes the intervals in `openchuck-app::operation::keep_out_of`.
- Tests in `crates/openchuck-app/tests/held_region.rs`, one per strategy that runs on a mesh part: `req_set_09_req_nfr_12_<strategy>_keeps_clear_of_the_held_region` for roughing, helical, axial, indexed and simultaneous finishing, rest machining and edge following. Each runs on round and on box stock, checks that no fed move's tool body overlaps a gripped length, and checks that the simulated stock keeps the whole held region. Each failed before the fix. Unit tests in `openchuck-path` cover the pass splitting, the drilled-hole rule, the refusal when nothing is left, and the refusal of an assembled move that reaches in. Pencil, drilling and swarf have no system test here (pencil needs a crease, the others a STEP part); the shared step covers them (OI-552).
- Projects with held regions whose finishing passes ran over them now have shorter passes there, and their G-code changes. The existing workholding tests and their checks are unchanged.
- An entry that runs back from a pass's start, such as a ramp, cannot start a pass that begins beside a held region, because it would run back over it. Generation then refuses with the entry's own message ("try another entry style"). On the owner's `blisk_1_overcut_mismatch` project, helical finishing with a ramp entry is refused, and with a plunge entry it generates. Before this record that pass began in the air beyond the stock's start, over the chuck (OI-553).
- The `cone_held_tabs` reference job's golden programs change. Before, their X ran from -12 to 42, over both held regions (X -12 to -8 and 40 to 42). Now they run from -6 to 38: each held region's inner edge, less the ball's 1.5 mm radius and the 0.5 mm clearance. They were regenerated with `OPENCHUCK_BLESS=1`, and no other golden file changed.
- Test `req_set_09_req_op_03_roughing_clears_the_stock_beside_the_held_region_all_round` (item 6). It fails without the turn at the end.
- Open items: OI-552, a system test of pencil, drilling and swarf next to a held region on a STEP part; OI-553, entries that run back from a pass beside a held region.
