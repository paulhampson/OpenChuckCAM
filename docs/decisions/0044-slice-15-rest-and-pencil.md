## Decision

1. **Remaining material is found through the existing `StockModel` port (design question, accepted by the slice).** Nothing about the previous tool is stored or passed: the stock an operation is generated against is the simulated stock that the operations before it left (SIM-06), so "areas the previous tool's radius could not reach" are exactly the places where that stock still stands above the target. `openchuck_path::rest::RemainingMap::detect` samples the target's tessellation no further apart than half the stepover or half the tool's radius (at least 0.02 mm; wider when the part would need more than 2 million samples) and keeps a sample when the stock holds **all five** of the points `threshold` out from it: along the normal and tilted 50 degrees from it towards each side along and across the face. That approximates the closest distance to empty space that the simulation's deviation reports (SIM-05, [0025](0025-slice-7-simulation.md) item 6), so the strategy and the report agree on what "material left" means. A single probe along the normal was tried first and rejected: at a corner an earlier tool rounded off, the stock stands thick along the face normal while the nearest empty space is a fraction of a millimetre to the side, so a second run kept finding "material" the report did not show.
2. **The threshold is `min_remaining`, default 0.15 mm, 0 meaning the whole part.** It is measured above the part plus its stock to leave. 0.15 mm is more than one simulation cell at the default 0.1 mm, because the stock is only known to a cell (the same reason as roughing's `min_cut`, OI-152, which this does not settle).
3. **`rest_machining` is helical finishing with the new tool, cut only where material is left.** It has the helical parameters (`rotation`, `start_angle`, `max_angle_step` default 2 degrees, stepover, scallop is not offered) and `min_remaining`. A point of the helix is cut when a sample with material left lies within **half a stepover plus one sample spacing** of its cutter contact; the limits of each wanted stretch are found by bisection (the path fitter's existing region-edge search, with a `wanted` predicate on `Fit` instead of a region constraint), so a pass starts and ends where the material does and the shared services link the pieces (OP-14). The tool is dropped on the whole part, so cutting part of a helix cannot gouge the rest. It does not honour selections yet (OI-190) and refuses an operation that has them, as other strategies do.
4. **Ordering and dependency (slice item 4) need nothing new.** An operation's key chains from the one before it (decision 0030 item 1), so editing the earlier operation makes the rest operation again and editing the rest operation leaves the earlier one alone (`req_op_09_req_sim_06_rest_machining_after_an_operation_is_generated_on_its_stock`). A disabled operation is outside the chain (decision 0030, amendment), so disabling the roughing makes the rest operation cut what the roughing would have.
5. **`pencil_finishing` follows concave creases.** `openchuck_path::pencil::concave_creases` finds the edges of the part's tessellation between two faces that turn at least `crease_angle` (default 30 degrees) and meet at a concave angle (the far corner of one triangle lies outside the plane of the other), welds vertices a hundred-thousandth of a millimetre apart so a mesh with a vertex per triangle still joins, and chains the edges into open or closed polylines at vertices with exactly two creases. Each chain is resampled at most `max_step` apart (default 0.5 mm). At a sample the tool rests where, searched across the crease in the plane it moves in, within 2.5 tool radii each way, its tip is lowest and nearest the crease: in a V that is the one place it touches both sides, and against a wall rising from a floor it is the place where it just touches the wall. The tool is only ever placed by a drop on the part with its stock to leave, so the pass cannot gouge (amended by [decision 0162](0162-simulation-results-histogram-and-probe.md) item 6: a placement cannot, but a move between two placements could, so each move is now checked as well). Unless `min_remaining` is 0, a stretch of the crease is cut only where the remaining-material map finds material near the contact. Passes are ordered by nearest end, reversing one when its far end is the nearer.
6. **The application says when nothing has cut before.** `Strategy::needs_earlier_operations` (a provided method, default false, so no host breaks) is true for both strategies. When the operation is generated against the setup's own stock because no earlier operation of its setup has been simulated, the operation's notes say that all of the part counts as having material left. The strategy still runs (the user may mean it), and with the setup's stock it cuts everywhere (`req_op_09_the_whole_part_is_rest_when_no_earlier_operation_has_cut`).
7. **Nothing to clean is an error with a message, like roughing's "no cut to make".** "Rest machining has nothing to clean: none of N points sampled on the part has more than T mm of material above it with its stock to leave of L mm. Run it after the operation whose leftovers it should cut, or lower min_remaining." Pencil says "no inside corner to follow" when the part has no crease at the angle, and "found material left but the tool cannot rest in any corner that holds it" when it finds some but cannot reach any. Whether an operation with nothing to clean should stop the whole regeneration is OI-191.
8. **A vertical tool's limits are recorded, not hidden.** Both strategies drop a vertical tool, so a wall parallel to the tool axis is reached only by the tool's equator at the foot and its dropped height above; a rest pass still lowers the worst remaining value there (1.86 mm to 0.35 mm on a stepped shaft with a true wall) but cannot clear it. Indexed or simultaneous rest forms are OI-189.
9. **No format or family change.** `rest` and `pencil` are new modules with new types (`RemainingMap`, `RestMachining`, `PencilFinishing`, `Crease`), the trait gains a provided method, `Registry::builtin` offers two more strategies, and `Fit` (crate-private) gains a field; no public struct gains a field, so `cargo-semver-checks` has nothing to flag and the family stays at 0.7.0. No project schema number is taken.

## Alternatives considered

- **A `previous_tool_diameter` parameter and a geometric reachability test** (the literal reading of "areas the previous tool's radius could not reach"): rejected. The carried stock already records where the previous tool did not reach, including where it was stopped by stock to leave, a selection or a gouge check, which a diameter cannot say. A stored diameter would also go stale when the earlier operation's tool changed.
- **A new port, `RemainingMaterial`, implemented by the simulator:** rejected for now. The library may not depend on the simulator and `contains` on the port is all the detection needs; the cost is OI-193.
- **A heightfield difference between stock and target** (as roughing's `HeightStock`): rejected for rest. Next to a wall the height difference reads as the wall's height and the cell quantisation reads as a large error; the probes of item 1 do not.
- **Making pencil a mode of rest machining:** rejected. They share the map and the threshold, but pencil is not a surface-following pass and its parameters (crease angle, step) are its own.
- **Honouring selections now:** deferred (OI-190); the mask would have to combine with `RegionConstraint` in the path fitter, which lane L is changing for roughing (slice 14 item 5).
