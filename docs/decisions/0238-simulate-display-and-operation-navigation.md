## Decision

1. **One stale message (297).** Keep one actionable viewport warning: stale mark, “Simulation out of date”, existing reason, and “Run simulation again” without an operation number. Remove repeated warnings/buttons from Playback and the inspector. Retain the step-bar mark, dimmed old results/playback, “Results of the out-of-date simulation:” identification, and export blocking. Disabled/deleted operations still drop playback. With no enabled operation, retain guidance without a button. Explicitly skipped stock simulation retains the accurate toolpath-only action/wording of accepted 0192 item 3. This records the existing owner direction as the owner-accepted amendment to 0192.
2. **Colour visibility (293).** “Deviation colours” lives in the results inspector and works for current and stale simulated results. Off shows plain cut stock without clearing staleness or hiding its warning. SIM-15 filters retain their meanings and accepted 0216 hidden-band slate. Verify the earlier warning/control fix through whole-dock checks.
3. **Shared Show state (299).** Quick boxes and the Show menu share Part, Cut part and Toolpaths. Model becomes Part; generic Stock is confirmed to be the same simulated-stock object as Cut part. Protect the last main choice in both controls; default/all-off recovery is Cut part. Axes, Held regions and tabs, and Selections remain menu-only. Overall Toolpaths masks operation paths and their faint trace; each tree eye retains its own saved choice when the overall mask is lifted.
4. **Opacity and surface values (300).** The Show menu offers Translucent and Solid, remembered per user; Translucent is the default. Plain stock retains 30% opacity. Mapped colours use 98% opacity in translucent mode: lower opacity failed rendered contrast/colour-vision checks at the accepted palette's narrow margins. Solid uses 100% opacity and depth occlusion. Internal geometry still contributes through translucent mapped stock. No renderer restructuring or project-format change is required.

   Existing colours/values belonged to target-model triangles, which opaque stock would obscure. The owner explicitly authorised display-only mapping/picking on cut stock (“Yes. Proceed with proposed approach”, 24 Oct 2026; OI-988). Project the nearest counted final-comparison sample to each stock triangle's centre, retaining its signed value and bound. Beyond the measurement search distance plus one cell, show Not measured. Equal-distance ties use original sample order. Build the spatial index, mesh, mapping and picker off the UI thread. Colours and picking use the same stock-triangle IDs. Tooltips say “Final result · … (nearest target sample)”; they do not imply a new measurement at the current playback position. A nearer visible model/stock face hides hover and pinned values. Clear pins when mesh identities change. Histograms, worst values and simulation calculations stay unchanged.
5. **Operation navigation (302).** The owner accepted single-click or Enter on a focused Simulate operation row opening Operations with that operation selected and its inspector visible. Enable, visibility, reorder and duplicate controls do not navigate. Reuse completed BUG-EDIT Keep/Discard/Cancel before selection/departure; Cancel preserves draft, selection, step and committed inputs. Pause on leaving Simulate. Preserve cursor and Simulate camera for the same available run on return; reset its window under 0169. Operation edits correctly mark the retained result stale. A new run starts normally; disabling/deleting retains the run-drop rule.
6. **Preparation and grouping (305).** Show the existing pipeline's monotonic progress directly below the position bar on first loading and regeneration, using its reported percentage without a time estimate. Toolpath-only runs say “Preparing toolpaths”. Stages without a stable total show activity without a percentage. Remove progress on readiness/failure; draw a disabled position bar before a timeline exists. Put “Turn part with A” immediately beside “Skip stock simulation”, with a thin divider only, no group name/box. Group speed/path-display choices where width permits, retain target sizes and use denser Playback row gaps. Preserve fixed dock bounds and existing short-dock scrolling.
