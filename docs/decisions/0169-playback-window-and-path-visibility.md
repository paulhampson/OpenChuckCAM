# 0169: The playback window and each operation's toolpath visibility (lane BUG-SIM; issues 205, 213)

- **Status:** proposed (lane BUG-SIM, 21 Oct 2026). Only the project owner accepts it (decision 0049). It amends the interface specification sections 6 and 11.5 (item 7 gives the proposed text; the specification is not edited until the owner agrees).
- **Date:** 21 Oct 2026.
- **Open items:** OI-601 answered by the owner (relayed by the coordinator, 21 Oct 2026) and closed; OI-604 new. Project schema 15 is shared with [decision 0168](0168-simulate-controls-and-stock-simulation.md).

## Context

Trial feedback in work package 3, with the owner's answers in the issue comments:

- **#205 (F-49).** A resizable, draggable window on the playback bar, so the view shows one section of a long path. Owner: the window also limits playback (the play head runs only inside it); it can be set by numbers (start and end) as well as by dragging; it is not remembered (it resets); the path outside it is drawn faintly by default, and a tick box hides it completely, which is the cheaper mode for slow graphics (#200). The lane chooses the wording (X7b, decision 0114), and says what the faint trace costs and what hiding saves. Asked through the coordinator: the tick box is remembered (OI-601, option A). It must not change the simulation's results or the G-code (GC-07, NFR-06).
- **#213 (F-57).** A visibility toggle (an eye) for each operation's toolpath. Owner: in every step that shows the tree, not only Simulate; remembered and saved with the project (a schema claim); a show-all and hide-all control for the group. It is separate from enabling (UI-06) and changes nothing in the output; its state is clear without colour (UI-11) and it is reachable from the keyboard.

## Decision

1. **The window is a range of moves** (`playback::MoveWindow`, first and last move, both included, counted from 0 across all operations; shown from 1). `Playback::set_window` puts the ends in order, brings them inside the program, treats the whole program as no window, and moves the play head inside. Play, advance, step, jump to a move or an operation, and seek all keep the play head inside: playing stops at the window's end and starts again at its start. The stock at the play head and the deviation colours are the simulation's own, unchanged (GC-07): the window limits only what is drawn and where the play head may go.
2. **On the playback bar.** An 8-pixel strip under the timeline shows the window as a band with a handle at each end (the whole program when there is none). Dragging an end resizes it (past the other end, they swap), dragging the band moves it with its length kept, and dragging elsewhere starts a window where the button was pressed. On the "Go to move" row, "Window: moves [from] to [to]" sets it by numbers from the keyboard, and "Whole program" lifts it. Not remembered: a new run, opening a project, and leaving the Simulate step lift it.
3. **Drawing.** With a window, the toolpath layer holds only the moves inside it (`ToolpathDisplay::window_lines`), cut at the moves' ends so the drawing starts and stops where the play head can, at full detail when the window's points fit the point budget and simplified otherwise. The rest of the path is a faint trace (`trace_lines`): the whole visible path at 1 mm (`TRACE_LEVEL`, or coarser when the budget needs it), from the level cache, in one grey, see-through hairline batch (one draw call) in its own viewport layer, `Layer::Trace`, drawn under the toolpaths. It is made once per path and set of visible operations, never again while the window moves or resizes. "Hide path outside window" (the lane's wording) removes the trace, so only the window's moves are drawn. The tick box is disabled until a window is set.
4. **What it costs** (#200). Measured on the owner's `blisk_slow_toothpath_rendering` project (issue 200's attachment, one rotary roughing operation of 23 311 moves, 47 826 points; release build, this container's CPU; no graphics adapter, so frame times are not measured, OI-604):
   - the whole path, no window: 47 826 points, drawn as wide lines;
   - the trace at 1 mm: 29 384 points as hairlines (2 vertices a segment, against the wide lines' 6), made in 3 ms the first time and 1.4 ms from the cache; 0.3 mm kept 45 013, so the coarser level was chosen;
   - a window of 10% of the moves: 3 900 points; faint mode draws those plus the trace (33 284 points, about 70% of the whole path's, most of them as cheaper hairlines); hidden mode draws 3 900, 92% fewer than the whole path; a 1% window, 361 points, 99% fewer. Making a window's lines took 0.05 to 0.4 ms.
   The faint mode is not the fix for slow graphics, as the owner noted; the hidden mode is the saving, and the trace is uploaded once rather than with every drag.
5. **"Hide path outside window" is remembered between runs** as a per-user setting (`SimulateSettings::hide_outside_window`), unticked by default (the faint trace): the owner's answer to OI-601. The window itself is not remembered.
6. **Each operation's toolpath has an eye in the tree, in every step** (#213). An open eye while the path is drawn, the same eye struck through while it is hidden: drawn as shapes, so the state reads without colour and never depends on a font's glyph (issue 177). It is a button the keyboard reaches (Tab, then Space or Enter), with the tooltip "Show or hide this operation's toolpath in the view. Nothing it makes changes", and it tells a screen reader its state. The Operations header has "Show all toolpaths" and "Hide all toolpaths" as an open and a struck eye beside its "+", each disabled while there is nothing to do. On Simulate the line under the tree says "Hiding a toolpath changes the view only. The toolpaths, the simulation and the G-code do not change." Each change is one undoable step (`Command::SetPathsShown`, one or all operations).
   - **Saved with the project**: `project.toml` gains `hidden_paths`, the operations whose path is hidden, in machining order, written only when one is (schema 15, decision 0168). The project keeps the choice for an operation it no longer has, so undoing a deletion restores it, but writes only the operations it has.
   - **No input to anything made** (GC-07, NFR-06): the set lives on the project, not on the operations, so it never reaches an operation's cache key; a hidden operation is generated, simulated and posted as before, and stays up to date. `Project::same_output` lets the export dialog keep its files when only this changes. Test `req_gc_07_req_nfr_06_hiding_a_path_changes_no_toolpath_or_g_code`.
   - The viewport's "Show or hide" (V) still hides all toolpaths at once in the view only, and Simulate's "Toolpath" tick box (decision 0168) likewise; the eyes are the per-operation, saved choice.
7. **Proposed specification edits** (not made until the owner agrees):
   - 6, the row columns: item 7 becomes "A row action: progress and cancel while running, or the visibility eye on operations (every step; open, or struck through when hidden; saved with the project)"; and the group headers: "The Operations header also has Show all and Hide all toolpaths (an open and a struck eye)".
   - 11.5, Tree: "Each operation has an eye button that shows or hides its toolpath in the view only, in every step (section 6). Simulation results and the G-code do not change when something is hidden; a line under the tree says so."
   - 11.5, the playback dock: add "**Window:** a strip under the timeline with a band and two handles: drag an end to resize, the band to move, elsewhere to start one; 'Window: moves [from] to [to]' and 'Whole program' on the Go to move row. The play head runs only inside it. The rest of the path is a faint grey trace unless 'Hide path outside window' is ticked (kept per user). Not remembered: leaving Simulate, a new run or opening a project lifts it."
