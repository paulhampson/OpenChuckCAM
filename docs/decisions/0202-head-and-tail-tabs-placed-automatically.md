# 0202: Head and tail tabs, placed automatically, and a rounded new tab diameter (lane SETUPPOS; issues #182, #184 and #186)

- **Status:** proposed (lane SETUPPOS, 21 Oct 2026). The behaviours in items 1 to 5 are the owner's answers on the issues (relayed in their comments, 20 Oct 2026); what the buttons make (item 2) is OI-780, sent to the coordinator for the owner and built here as the lane's recommendation (option A) until answered.
- **What it amends:** the interface specification's section 11.7 (edited with this record), and [decision 0035](0035-workholding-and-retaining-tabs.md) and [decision 0041](0041-slice-21a-workholding-data.md) (a tab gains `auto`). No requirement changes: OP-21 and SET-09 already cover tabs and their zones; the buttons and the tick box are interface functions on them.
- **Date:** 21 Oct 2026.
- **Open items:** OI-780 (what "Head tab" and "Tail tab" make) is new and open, waiting for the owner.

## Context

Trial feedback F-26, F-28 and F-30. Issue #182 asks for quick-create buttons for a head tab and a tail tab, placed from where the part sits in the stock. Issue #184: the diameter a new tab gets is not rounded. Issue #186 asks for a tick box on each head and tail tab that recalculates it from the part position, the workholding and the stock size. The owner answered on the issues: the buttons use the existing tab defaults; with no room they are disabled with a tooltip saying why; a second press adds another tab. The diameter is rounded to the section's field precision, and only the diameter. The tick box is per tab, and a tab made by the buttons starts ticked. Position and length are calculated, the diameter is not. With no room the tab shows a warning and is not blocked or removed. A hand edit of a calculated value unticks it.

These rest on the part's position being right, which [decision 0201](0201-setup-offset-moves-the-part.md) makes so: the zones follow the setup's offset. Requirements: OP-21, SET-09, UI-25, UI-07, GC-07 and NFR-06 (the same inputs give the same tab), NFR-07, NFR-12. Records read: 0035 (OI-158, the owner's tab defaults), 0041, 0045, 0104 (the workholding section), 0160 and 0161 (held regions), 0114 (wording).

## Decision

1. **A tab can be placed automatically (project schema 17).** A tab gains the optional `auto`, `"head"` or `"tail"` (`openchuck_types::workholding::TabEnd`), written only when set. While it is set, the tab's position and length are the whole zone at that end of the part (`auto_tab_span`): from the held region's inner edge, or the start of the stock, to the part at the head, and from the part to the other held region, or the end of the stock, at the tail, so the tab joins the part to stock that is kept. This is the length the owner chose for the default tabs in OI-158. Its shape, diameters and layout stay as given.
2. **"Head tab" and "Tail tab" (issue #182; OI-780 open).** Two buttons beside "Add tab" in the Retaining tabs section. Each adds a tab placed automatically at that end, ticked. As built, pending OI-780: the shape and diameter of "Add tab" (a cylinder on the axis, a quarter of the stock's diameter, item 4), the position and length of item 1. The other options put to the owner were B, the zone default of "Add default tabs" for that end (a frustum sized from the part), and C, "Add tab" exactly, 5 mm long against the part (not recommended: it may not reach stock that is kept). A button is disabled with a tooltip when the setup has no part ("Import a model first") or no stock, while the part is being read, or when there is no room at that end ("No room at the head: the held region reaches the part." or "No room at the head: the stock starts at the part."). Each press adds another tab.
3. **Worked out wherever used.** `Workholding::resolved(stock, part)` gives the workholding with each automatic tab placed; every consumer of tabs as solids goes through it (`workholding::labelled_solids`, so the strategies' target, the keep-out, the simulation's comparison target, the view, the Setup panes and the checks) and `check_tab_zones`. The same part, stock and held regions always give the same tab (GC-07). The stored values are those at the last apply: the section stores the resolved values when it applies, and the draft takes them too, so it matches what is stored. Because a stored position can be left behind by a later change of stock or part, the data checks (`validate_workholding`, `check_draft`) do not hold an automatic tab's stored position against the stock, the held regions or other tabs; its resolved position is in its zone by construction. The cache key needs nothing new: it holds the setup and the part's file and placement, which fix the resolved tab.
4. **The new tab diameter is rounded (issue #184).** "Add tab" rounds its diameter (a quarter of the stock's diameter) to the 3 places the section's fields show (`to_field_places`), so the stored value is what the field shows. Its position and length are unchanged, as the owner said. Rounding changes only what a new tab gets; no stored file changes, so the file format is not affected.
5. **The tick box (issue #186).** Each tab's editor has a "Place automatically" check box. Ticked, the tab follows the part; it is enabled for a tab already automatic or lying wholly in the head or the tail zone, and otherwise disabled with the tooltip "Only a tab at the head or the tail of the part can be placed automatically." Unticking keeps the position the tab is shown at. Editing Start X or Length by hand unticks it, keeping the other value as shown; the diameters stay editable without unticking. The tab's header ends "· Automatic", and its Start X and Length show the resolved values.
6. **No room is a warning (issue #186).** When an automatic tab finds no room at its end (the held region, or the end of the stock, reaches the part), it keeps its last position, the setup checks and the Messages say so (`SetupWarningKind::AutoTabNoRoom`: "tab 2 is placed automatically at the head of the part, but there is no room there: ... It keeps its last position; move the part or shorten the held region, or untick "Place automatically" and place it by hand"), and the tab's row shows the warning. It is not blocked or removed.

## Alternatives considered

- **Store the auto values only, worked out when the setup changes.** The part can change without the setup changing (a new placement step, a replaced file), so the stored values would go stale with nothing to update them; working them out where they are used keeps them right.
- **No stored flag (derive "automatic" from a tab filling its zone).** A manual tab that happens to fill its zone would then move without the user asking.
- **Round every computed tab value.** The owner asked for the diameter only.

## Consequences

- `openchuck-types` (breaking with 0201's change): `Tab` gains the public field `auto`; `TabEnd`, `MIN_TAB_ROOM`, `auto_tab_span`, `Workholding::resolved`, `Workholding::auto_tabs_without_room` and `SetupWarningKind::AutoTabNoRoom` are new. Library family 0.15.0.
- `openchuck-project`: project schema 17 (shared with 0201); a schema 16 file with a tab holding `auto` is refused, with what to do.
- `openchuck-app`: `workholding_view::end_tab`, `no_room`, `to_field_places`, `set_tab_span`, `set_tab_auto`; `labelled_solids` resolves; the draft checks skip an automatic tab's stored position.
- `openchuck-ui`: the two buttons, the check box, the header mark, the warning on the row.
