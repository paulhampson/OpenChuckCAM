## Context

Slice 21c connects the setup's `[workholding]` data (21a, merged) to the composite target (21b, merged) in the application, and to the simulation, the deviation report, the viewport, the forms and the command line. Decision 0035 fixes the design (the held region and the tabs are part of the target, so no strategy changes) and the owner's answers on defaults, tab zones and the tool. This record states the concrete choices the wiring needed.

## Decision (proposed)

1. **Mapping.** `openchuck-app::workholding` builds the analytic solids from the setup (`labelled_solids`): the held region at the start, the one at the end, then each tab (a tab around the axis as `count` solids by ascending angle). Positions are model X, the held region and a tab on the axis are about the rotary axis, and a tab around the axis is parallel to it at its radial offset, the angle measured from +Y towards +Z (decision 0041 item 4). `generate_operation_on` joins them to the model's surface with `CompositeSurface` and passes that as `StrategyInput::surface`. The part's own bounds still feed the analytic stock and `part_bounds` (bounding-box stock and the simulation's grid must not grow with the solids). A setup with no held region and no tab uses the model's `MeshSurface` as before, so its G-code is byte for byte unchanged (the existing golden files are unchanged).
2. **A held region on stock that is not a cylinder needs a holding diameter.** A held region is a cylinder: of the holding diameter where one is set, else of the stock's own diameter. A box, bounding-box-offset or imported stock has no single diameter, so a held region on it without a holding diameter is refused at generation with a message saying so, and a holding diameter wider than the stock's smaller side is refused. *Choice a reviewer may change* (OI-195): the alternative is the inscribed circle (which would cut the box's corners at the grip, wrongly), or a solid for a box section (not built).
3. **Default tabs** (decision 0035 item 4, owner's answers; OI-158) are computed on request, as explicit data to add to the setup, not applied silently at generation: `Application::start_default_tabs` returns the tabs, and the form or `openchuck-cli setup --default-tabs` stores them as one undoable edit, so the stored data stays explicit (decision 0041 item 2) and a project without tabs stays unchanged. For each of the head zone and tail zone (the part's extent against the stored held regions, `tab_zones`) that has length, one tab runs the whole zone, a frustum on the axis with its small end on the part. The owner fixed the length, the small end ("the smaller of 10 mm and half the part's diameter at the tab's position") and the shape; this record fixes the rest:
   - *Local diameter.* The part's section by the plane where the tab meets it (the farthest point from the axis of the mesh edges crossing the plane). Half the local diameter is the radius, so the small end's diameter is the smaller of 10 mm and the local radius. A part that ends in a point there has no default tab (the message says to add it by hand).
   - *Large end.* Twice the small end, at most the stock's diameter. The owner did not give it. *Choice a reviewer may change* (OI-198).
   - *Excluded regions.* The part never lies in the head or tail zones, and an avoid selection set (OP-02) holds triangles of the part, so a default tab can never enter one; no code is needed.
   - A zone with no length (the held region reaches the part, or the stock ends at it) gets no tab. Defaults are refused when the setup already has tabs.
4. **Checks that need the part and the tool** (OI-159 and OI-180, closed): `check_for_tool` runs before an operation is generated and refuses, naming the tab, a tab not wholly in the head or tail zone, and a tab whose narrowest diameter is smaller than the tool's cutting diameter (`2 x cutting_radius`). The forms run the same checks on the draft before storing it (`Application::check_workholding`), so a bad edit is refused when made; `check_setups` adds a note per tab narrower than a tool of the setup's operations. The command line only warns when editing (decision 0041 item 9); generation refuses.
5. **Simulation.** The simulated stock starts as the setup's stock definition and the strategies never cut the solids, so the stock keeps them. Nothing extra is simulated. The tests assert it: no rapid or fed move removes material inside a solid.
6. **Deviation.** The report compares the stock with the part and the solids as one surface. `deviation_target` builds it: the part's triangles first, with their indices, then the triangles of each solid at 96 segments, with every triangle that lies inside the union turned into a degenerate one so it takes no samples (inside another part, or on its surface with the faces opposed, as where a tab meets the part). The colours of the part's triangles read the first entries. The decision is made at the triangle's centroid: exact for a face wholly covered or wholly clear, approximate where a face is partly covered (OI-196). A held region or tab then counts as part where it shows, and an operation that cuts one is an overcut with a location.
7. **Selections that reach a held region or tab** (UI-03) are a warning, not a refusal: a selection set the operation cuts in (role `selections`) with a triangle corner inside or on a solid gets a note in the regeneration notes and under the operation's form (`start_workholding_warnings`, a job). They are never cut, so the selection cuts less than it names.
8. **Viewport and forms** (UI-03, UI-07). The scene gains `SceneRole::HeldRegion` and `SceneRole::Tab`, shaded beside the part. Selecting a setup opens the workholding form: held regions (length, optional diameter), each tab (shape, position, length, diameters, on or around the axis), the reason a draft cannot be stored under it, "Add tab", "Add default tabs", "Check against the part and tools", "Apply" (one undoable edit) and "Revert".
9. **Command line.** `openchuck-cli setup` gains `--held-start`, `--held-end` (`LENGTH` or `LENGTH,DIAMETER` or `none`), repeatable `--tab` (`cylinder=X_START,LENGTH,DIAMETER` or `frustum=X_START,LENGTH,START_DIAMETER,END_DIAMETER`, with `@COUNT,START_ANGLE,RADIAL_OFFSET` for tabs around the axis), `--clear-tabs` and `--default-tabs`.
10. **The example in the golden set** is `cone_held_tabs`: the cone in stock as wide as its wide end, held 4 mm and 2 mm, with a cylinder tab at the head and a frustum tab at the tail that continue the outline, so the target has no wall for the tool to climb. A wall (a tab narrower than the part's end face, or a shoulder against a wider held region) makes the helical path fitter bisect down to micro-moves, and the first trial, of a layout like the owner's default (a tab with its small end on the part's wide end face), failed gates 1 and 2 on them (OI-194). The golden audit checks the file against the exact outline, exempting the contact feed within 2.5 mm of the concave kink where the tab meets the part's narrow end.
11. **Verification.** The independent checker (surface of revolution) is applied to the part with its on-axis held regions and tabs: no pose of roughing or finishing goes more than 0.02 mm into it. Tabs around the axis are not a surface of revolution, so the simulation checks them (OI-197).

## Alternatives considered

- **Apply the default tabs at generation when none are set.** It would change the G-code of every project without tabs, which the checklist forbids, and hide stored data from the user.
- **Subtract the solids from the stock.** Rejected in decision 0035.
- **Filter deviation samples inside the union instead of degenerate triangles.** It would break the sample order that `triangle_values` and the colours rely on.
- **Refuse a selection that reaches a solid.** The solids lie mostly outside the part; a selection that touches one (a held region reaching into the part) is the user's intent to cut less. A warning says so.

## Open items

- **OI-194** (important): a wall in the target makes helical finishing produce micro-moves that fail gates 1 and 2.
- **OI-195:** held regions on box, bounding-box or imported stock need a holding diameter.
- **OI-196:** the deviation target decides interior faces at the centroid, inside corners the tool cannot reach read as material left, and rest machining and pencil clean-up were not tried against the composite, whose tessellation keeps the faces inside the union.
- **OI-197:** the independent checker covers surfaces of revolution only; tabs around the axis are checked by the simulation.
- **OI-198:** the default tab's large end, and the reading of "half the part's diameter" as the local radius, are this record's choices.
