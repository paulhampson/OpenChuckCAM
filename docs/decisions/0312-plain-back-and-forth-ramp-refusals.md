## Context

The owner reported (issue #291, 29 Oct 2026) that turning on the back-and-forth ramp gave "back-and-forth ramp needs whole-target, complete-tool and machine clearance inputs", which says nothing the user can act on.

The lane reproduced it on the owner's project (operation Roughing: rotary roughing, Entry Ramp, Back-and-forth ramp on, on a STEP part): `main` stops after 1.0 s with that text. The application gives the ramp's clearance check its inputs only for a mesh (STL) part (`operation.rs`, `placed.brep.is_none()`). This is deliberate: PR #340 lists "the option currently refuses unsupported STEP/body certification", and 0242 item 5 refuses when the representation cannot establish the bound. A STEP part's triangles are made at a nominal deflection with no proved largest error from the exact faces (as for facing, `projection_error: None`). Nothing in the setup, tool or machine fixes it. The defect was the wording, and that the user learned of it only when generating.

The other refusals of the ramp named internal checks ("clearance budget", "whole target", "complete-tool envelope") or gave no field to change, and the approved corridor refusal gave one message for every cause (the finished part, a held region or tab, the machine's travel or A range, the check limit).

## Decision

1. **STEP parts (owner's choice D1 = C).** On a STEP part the back-and-forth ramp stays refused, and no clearance rule changes. One check (`openchuck-app` `ramp_entry`) says so before Generate, in three places as the STEP set refusals are ([decision 0209](0209-step-geometry-for-every-strategy.md) item 10): an amber line under the Back-and-forth ramp field (Entry, links and retract), the operation in error with the line in Messages, and generation, with the same words: "The back-and-forth ramp is not available on a STEP part yet: its clearance cannot be checked against the part's exact faces. To generate this operation, in the operation's Entry, links and retract settings, turn off Back-and-forth ramp or set Entry to Plunge, Lead or Helix." The same check says when the tool's shank is wider than its cutter (0242 item 5's convex bound), naming the tool and both sizes. Proper STEP support is a follow-up lane (OI-1270).
2. **The corridor refusal (owner's choice D2 = A).** The approved first sentence is kept word for word; the second is replaced by the cause and the fix: "Back-and-forth ramp needs at least {minimum} mm of usable tool-centre travel; only 0.000 mm could be verified: within one tool diameter behind the first cut, {cause}. {fix}" The cause is one of: the tool would cut into the finished part by more than the tolerance (fix: another entry; a smaller tool needs a shorter run); it would reach a held region or a retaining tab (Setup step, Workholding and tabs); it would go beyond the machine's X, Y or Z travel (Setup step, Machine, Travel, or move the part with Offset along X); the A angle is outside the machine's A range (Setup step, Machine, A range); the check limit of 100000 checks for one entry (another entry); and, for the cases no setting changes, the tool's shape or a move that could not be checked (another entry; the last also asks for a defect report).
3. **A pass that starts above the stock (owner's choice D3 = A).** Still refused, reworded: "Pass {n} starts {h} mm above the stock, so the back-and-forth ramp has nothing to ramp down into. In the operation's Entry, links and retract settings, turn off Back-and-forth ramp or set Entry to Plunge."
4. **Every other refusal** of the ramp names the pass, what is wrong, the step, section and field to change, and what to try, with no internal names: a pass with no straight line (a single point, or one that only turns the part), a start above the height the tool is at before the pass (Safe Z for the first pass, else Safe radius or Safe Z by the Retract setting, or Approach distance), too many runs or too small a descent per run (Ramp angle), and a refusal on a later run, reversal, approach or connection (the causes of item 2). Library hosts that give no clearance inputs get a plain sentence saying so.

## Evidence

On the owner's project (numbers only; the project is kept outside the repository): `main` refuses in 1.0 s with the old text; with this change Regenerate refuses in 0.006 s, before the part is read, with item 1's text. With the option off the single-line ramp refuses at pass 200 (a 114.547 mm run to descend 6.003 mm at 3.00 degrees would cut 4.952 mm into the part), the helix at pass 200 likewise (4.956 mm), and Plunge generates in 3.1 s. A local diagnostic build that allowed the ramp on the STEP part's triangles (never committed) certified passes 1 to 98 and refused pass 99, a single point (OI-1271). The owner chose D4 = C (relayed by the coordinator, 29 Oct 2026): a second option, off by default, will enter such a pass by the existing plunge; it is not built here and needs its own record (follow-up lane STEPRAMP, with OI-1270).
