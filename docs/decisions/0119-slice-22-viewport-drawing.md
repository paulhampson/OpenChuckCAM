## Context

Decision 0114 found the viewport partly built against specification section 7: the toolbar's controls in the View menu (C5), one of the five overlays (C6), the scene in the old colours (C7, C8; D1 still open), no dash or hatch line styles (C8), and the held regions and tabs of 7.5 drawn with world-space hatch lines, hairline end rings and no hover or selection (C9, OI-334). Decision 0112 built wide lines; decision 0114 answer 6 set one width scale (OI-341): plain lines 1 pixel, role outlines 1.5, hovered 2, selected 3.

Requirements: UI-02, UI-03, UI-04, UI-25, SIM-01, SIM-03, SIM-05, IMP-04, NFR-03 and NFR-15. No requirement, architecture or project format changes; no engine change (slice 22 item 4).

## Decision

1. **The boards first (OI-342).** `Main.dc.html` (images 03, 13 and 14) gains the selection tools box of decision 0066: the Pick filter (Both, Faces, Edges), Select one, Flood fill with Chain, By angle with Smooth chain, Same direction and Box, and a hovered edge 2 pixels wide. Its path legend sits below the box and collapses to a button when the view is under 480 pixels high, which it is at 1366 by 768 (7.2). `HeldRegionsView.dc.html` (image 17) draws the selected outline 3 pixels and the hovered one 2. Rendered as `docs/ui-design/README.md` says, in headless Chromium with IBM Plex from Google Fonts (the host was reachable); the unchanged boards render pixel-identical to their committed images, so the method matches.
2. **Line styles in the renderer.** `LineItem` gains a `LineStyle`: solid, dashed (5 on, 4 off), dash-dot (14, 4, 2, 4) or dotted (2 on, 4 off), in logical pixels, from the boards' patterns. Each line point carries its distance along the line in millimetres, and the fragment shader turns it into pixels with the scale at the camera's target (`Frame::pixels_per_mm`). The dashes keep their size as the view zooms and keep their rhythm round a polyline; in perspective they shrink with depth, like the line itself. Solid lines take the same path with an empty pattern.
3. **A screen-space hatch.** `MeshItem` gains a `Hatch` (one direction, or crossed): lines 1 logical pixel wide and 6 apart, computed from the fragment's position on the screen, so their spacing does not change with the zoom or the screen's pixel density (`Frame::pixels_per_point`). Opaque, translucent and highlight meshes each have a hatched pipeline; meshes without a hatch keep the old ones, so the 1M-triangle frame is unchanged in kind (item 9).
4. **Outlines.** `MeshItem` gains an `Outline` (colour, width, style) and `outline_only`. On upload the renderer joins the mesh's coincident vertices and lists its edges with the normals of the two faces at each; an edge is drawn by the vertex shader when one face looks toward the eye and the other away (the silhouette), and always when it is a crease (more than 30 degrees, the feature-edge angle) or open. The outline is a wide line of the given width, so the 1.5, 2 and 3 pixel marks of 7.5 are one mechanism. A new layer, `Layer::Focus`, holds the hovered and selected solids' outlines, so pointing at them never uploads the scene again.
5. **Colour roles (C7, C8; D1, D12).** `apps/openchuck-ui/src/roles.rs` holds every role colour of 7.4 and 11.5 and the width scale. D1 is done: the rotary axis and the A+ arrow are cyan `#35d0e0`, and the import dialog's words follow ("the cyan line is the rotary axis"). The stock outline is dashed, the rotary axis dash-dot, the work axes 2-pixel arrows, holes ringed amber 2 pixels wide (a new `SceneRole::Hole`, so they read apart from the other findings), and the selected operation's avoid and check sets red with a single hatch. Toolpaths: cut amber and entry lilac at 2 pixels, link green at 1 (thinner), rapids grey and dotted. The tool is a lilac tip (the cutter) on a grey body (the shank). The view is the dark gradient of 14.2 in both themes. Deviation (D12, until OI-194 is decided): decision 0040's six bands in the colours of 11.5: overcut `#a3157f`, on the surface `#9aa0a8`, material left in three blues by depth (`#b9d6ff`, `#7ca7e8` between the two given, `#3f78d1`), and not measured the grey with a single dark hatch, so the scale reads by lightness.
6. **Held regions and tabs (C9; OI-334 m, n).** A held region is violet at 35% with the screen cross-hatch and a 1.5-pixel violet outline; a tab is sand, opaque, with a 1.5-pixel dark outline. While picking and on Simulate both are outlines only in their role colour (sand for a tab), and while picking they cannot be hovered or clicked. Otherwise the pointer ray finds the nearest one in front of the part: it is outlined 2 pixels in hover yellow, with its tooltip ("Tab 2 · frustum · X 14.000 to 16.000 mm · Ø 4.000 to Ø 8.000 mm", "1 of 3 at 90.00°" for a tab around the axis, "Held region at the start · 6.000 mm · Ø 26.000 mm (stock)"); a click selects it with a 3-pixel `accent` outline and reports it to the shell; Esc clears it. A tab around the axis is one item, all its solids together. The view model names the solids (`SceneView::solids`, `SceneSolid`, `WorkholdingItem`) with their tooltips, and no longer gives the world-space hatch lines. **The draft (OI-334 k, drawing part):** `Viewport::set_workholding_draft` draws a draft instead of the stored workholding, with the "Showing unapplied workholding" chip, a failing held region or tab as its outline in `error` red, and default tabs offered as dashed sand outlines with no fill. Building the draft and its failures is the workholding inspector's (lane X4, OI-355).
7. **The toolbar (C5).** `viewport_toolbar.rs` draws the 44-pixel strip above the view, never over it, from the viewport's own state: display Shaded, Wire, Edges; projection Persp, Ortho; Front, Top and Iso on Model and Setup; Fit (F); Show or hide (V) with part, stock, rotary axis and work axes, held regions and tabs, selections, and toolpaths, and the line "Hiding changes only the view, never what is machined."; Show findings on Model; and Show: Stock, Part + path, Both on Simulate. `Viewport::show` draws the strip and the view, so the shell needs no change for it. Section, Measure and Turn part are P2 (UI-21) and not built. The View menu keeps its entries until lane X2 rewrites the menus.
8. **The overlays (C6).** `viewport_overlays.rs` draws the dark boxes of 7.2 in their places: the view cube (top right; a face clicked gives that standard view), the axis triad under it, the readout (bottom right, on Operations and Simulate: X, Y, Z to 3 places and A to 2, "Cursor on surface" or "Tool at move N", and the frame, "G54 work coordinates", from a new `SceneView::work_register`), the path legend (left, under the selection tools box when it shows; Cut, Link, Rapid, Entry, Selected, Avoid, then Held region and Tab when present; a "Legend" button under 480 pixels), and the colour scale (bottom left on Simulate, with "Held region (kept)" and "Tab (kept)" outline swatches under it). The legend no longer sits on every step. Until lane X2 builds the Setup legend strip, the held region and tab legend is drawn at the bottom left on Model and Setup, in the overlay's light text (the old one took the theme's text colour, C6). The playback panel's band and class rows are removed; the overlays replace them.
9. **The step and the shell.** The toolbar and overlays depend on the workflow step. Until lane X2 builds the steps, the view takes it from what it holds: Simulate when the part is coloured by deviation or the stock is shown, Operations while picking or when toolpaths are drawn, Model when the scene has findings, else Setup. `Viewport::set_step` overrides it. The only shell change is one call that tells the selection panel which operation is chosen, so its avoid and check sets are drawn (item 5).
10. **The frame budget (NFR-03).** Meshes without a hatch or outline draw through the same pipelines as before, and the uniform block grows from 112 to 160 bytes per draw. The 1M-triangle benchmark (`openchuck-ui --bench`, a 1,000,000-triangle sphere, shaded with edges, 15 s, release builds) was run twice each on `main` and on this branch on the same container, on lavapipe software Vulkan under Xvfb: p50 145 to 149 ms on `main` and 147 to 149 ms here, p95 205 to 208 ms on `main` and 195 to 199 ms here. Both fail the 33 ms limit in software, as expected; the figures compare the two and are not the reference-hardware result, which is lane X8's. Wide toolpaths cost six vertices a segment instead of two; the benchmark has no toolpath, so that is OI-356.

## Interface left for the other lanes (OI-355)

- **Lane X2 (shell):** `Viewport::set_step` once the steps exist; the Setup legend strip from `viewport_overlays::workholding_entries` and `swatch_for`; a message with "Edit workholding" when the setup's workholding cannot be built (the scene's note today); the View menu's display entries now duplicate the toolbar.
- **Lane X4 (inspector):** on `PickInput::solid_click`, select the setup and open the workholding section with the item expanded and its first field focused; `Viewport::set_focused_solid` for the row with keyboard focus; `Viewport::set_selected_solid` to clear or set the selection from the inspector; `Viewport::set_workholding_draft` with the draft's solids (from `workholding_view::display_items`), its failing items and the default tabs offered. These methods are marked as not yet used, so the build flags them once wired.
- **Lane X5 (Setup panes)** uses the line styles and the roles of `roles.rs` (clearance and safe Z green dashed, the travel limit red hatched).
- **Lane X6 (Simulate)** takes the deviation colours from `roles.rs` and moves per-operation visibility to the tree's eye buttons (OI-357).

## Alternatives considered

- **Dashes measured in world units.** Simple, but the dashes would grow and shrink with the zoom, and a dashed stock outline would turn solid or dotty when zoomed out. Rejected.
- **Dash phase restarted on each segment, in screen space.** Exact on screen, but a curve of short segments (the stock's end circles) shows no pattern at all. Rejected for the distance along the line.
- **Silhouettes from a screen-space edge filter (depth and normal discontinuities).** General, but it needs an extra pass and target in a renderer that draws inside egui's pass, and would outline the part too. Rejected for per-mesh outline edges.
- **Hatch as world-space lines (as built).** Its spacing changes with the zoom, against 7.5's 6 pixels on screen. Replaced.
- **Leave the toolbar to lane X2.** The strip's controls are viewport state, and 0114 gives the toolbar contents to this lane; drawing it in the viewport widget avoids editing `shell.rs` while X2 rewrites it. X2 may move it.

## Consequences

- `openchuck-viewport`: `LineStyle`, `Hatch`, `Outline`, `MeshItem::{hatch, outline, outline_only}`, `LineItem::style`, `Scene::add_styled_polyline`, `Layer::Focus`, `Frame::{pixels_per_point, pixels_per_mm}`. Breaking for a crate outside the library family, so no `cargo-semver-checks` run applies. Render tests for each, run on lavapipe (CI sets `OPENCHUCK_REQUIRE_ADAPTER`).
- `openchuck-app`: `SceneRole::Hole`, `SceneView::{solids, work_register}`, `SceneSolid`, `WorkholdingItem`; `workholding_view::display_items` returns the solids and no hatch lines.
- `openchuck-ui`: `roles.rs`, `viewport_toolbar.rs`, `viewport_overlays.rs`; the viewport, playback panel and selection panel use them; hovered triangle and face outlines are 2 pixels (OI-341).
- OI-334: items m and n and the drawing of k are built; the wiring of k, the layout and the Setup panes remain with lanes X4, X2 and X5. OI-342: this lane's boards are done.
