# Reference job set for the G-code gates

Chosen in work breakdown slice 6 item 12; recorded in [decision 0020](decisions/0020-slice-6-post-and-round-trip.md) item 8. The set is what gates 3, 4 and 5 of [verification plan section 6](verification-plan.md) run. **Generated G-code is the user's to verify before running on a machine (NFR-12); nothing here has been run on a Z1.**

## The jobs

All three use the same tool, material and strategy: a 3 mm ball end mill (2 flutes, 8 mm flute length), oak (12000 rpm, 0.02 mm per tooth, plunge 150 mm/min, stepover 2 mm), helical finishing with 10 degree steps, on the Makera Z1 profile. Stock is a cylinder 2 mm larger than the part in diameter and 2 mm longer. The parts are generated by `openchuck-testkit::parts`, so no model files are stored.

| Job | Part | What it exercises | Golden programs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `groove` | Cylinder, radius 12 mm, 30 mm long, with a helical groove (pitch 12 mm, 1.5 mm deep) | Simultaneous X and A with Z following the groove; about 15 turns of A, then the Z1 unwind (`G92.4 A0 S0`) | `testdata/golden/reference/groove_makera-z1.nc`, `groove_linuxcnc.ngc` |
| `cone` | Cone from radius 10 mm to 5 mm, 30 mm long | Z changing steadily with X; feed rule on a changing radius | `cone_*` |
| `sphere` | Sphere, radius 10 mm | Rim and pole behaviour; short moves | `sphere_*` |

`cargo test -p openchuck-app --test reference_jobs` posts each job with both shipped posts, requires gates 1 and 2 to pass, checks the output is identical at 1 and 4 threads, and compares it with the golden files (`OPENCHUCK_BLESS=1` rewrites them; review the diff). The operation id in the comment line is replaced by `<id>` in the golden files.

## Assumptions to settle before gates 4 and 5

- **Axis height.** The owner gave the chuck centre as 45 mm above the bed (13 Oct 2026). The Z1 profile takes the bed at the bottom of the Z travel (-100), so the axis is at machine Z -55, and the safe Z is 50 mm above the axis (5 mm below the top of the travel). That reading of "the bed" is an assumption (OI-60). **Measure the axis height on the machine and rerun the jobs before any trial.** A wrong value moves every Z.
- **Work zero.** Programs run in G54 with Y0 Z0 on the A axis and X0 at the part's model origin (OI-40). The operator sets X0 by hand.
- **A direction** was checked on the machine (13 Oct 2026): A+ turns the top of the chuck toward the front (-Y), as the profile says (OI-03). **Wrap, feed rule and unwind** (`G92.4 A0 S0`) are the derived SP-12 conventions, unconfirmed until the air cut (OI-37).
- **No tool changer test.** One tool, `T1 M6` at the start; the tool change is exercised in a separate attended test (OI-41).

## Gate 3

`cargo xtask replay` runs each LinuxCNC program (`testdata/golden/reference/*_linuxcnc.ngc` and the small part) in LinuxCNC's headless simulator and compares what it reached with the replay (`openchuck-cli gcode-sim`). Pass criterion: every move reached in order, no fault, the program ends where the replay says. **Result, LinuxCNC 2.9.10, 13 Oct 2026:**

| Program | Moves reached | Largest difference | Simulated motion | Faults |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sphere_linuxcnc.ngc` | 366 | 0.0667 | 86.0 s | none |
| `cone_linuxcnc.ngc` | 546 | 0.0513 | 115.8 s | none |
| `groove_linuxcnc.ngc` | 640 | 0.0472 | 149.2 s | none |

The simulator cannot confirm the A direction, or the Z1 post's `G92.4` and feed rule: the Z1 programs share the toolpath and the kinematics with these, and gate 4 checks the rest on the machine. See [tools/linuxcnc/README.md](../tools/linuxcnc/README.md) for how to run it and [docker/linuxcnc](../docker/linuxcnc/Dockerfile) for the image CI uses.

## Air-cut plan (gate 4) for the operator

For the operator to review before the first trial; amend it after the review. Rules: verification plan section 6 (attended, emergency stop in reach, no tool, no material, reduced feed).

1. Check the profile values the programs depend on: axis height (OI-60), travel (OI-39). The A direction is already confirmed. Regenerate the Z1 programs and compare with the golden files.
2. Run SP-12's conventions program first (`spikes/sp12/AIR-CUT.md` on the SP-12 branch) and record the results in decision 0007's open items (wrap, feed rule, G93; the direction is confirmed).
3. Run `sphere_makera-z1.nc` with feed override at 10 %: the shortest program and the smallest envelope. Watch the first 20 lines; stop on anything unexpected.
4. Time one simultaneous XYZA move and compare with the IR's predicted time (the program comment states the total).
5. Run `groove_makera-z1.nc` and `cone_makera-z1.nc` the same way; watch the unwind at the end (`G92.4 A0 S0`: A must not move).
6. Record, for each run: file, override, result, measured against predicted time, alarms. Any unexpected motion is a defect with the G-code attached.

Gate 5 (first cut in wax or foam) follows only when gate 4 passes, with the safe-practice checklist reviewed by the Z1's operator (slice 20).
