# OpenChuckCAM Entity Contributor Licence Agreement

Version 1.0, 11 October 2026.

Adapted from the Apache Software Foundation Software Grant and Corporate Contributor License Agreement (v r190612), with the same changes as the [individual agreement](CLA.md): the party receiving the licence is the OpenChuckCAM Maintainer, section 2 states the right to relicense, and section 9 adds the Maintainer's open source commitment.

This agreement lets an organisation (the "Entity") submit Contributions to the Maintainer, authorise Contributions made by its designated employees, and grant copyright and patent licences for them. Each designated employee also signs the [individual agreement](CLA.md). The reason for the agreement is explained there.

## How to sign

A person authorised to bind the Entity completes the details and signature below and sends the document to the Maintainer through the contact listed in the repository. The Maintainer records the Entity and its designated employees in the project's CLA records.
