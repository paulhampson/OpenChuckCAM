OpenCASCADE Technology notice and source offer

OpenChuckCAM reads STEP files with the OpenCASCADE Technology (OCCT) 7.8.1
geometry kernel. The kernel is statically linked into the program
"openchuck-step-worker" that ships beside the application, and into nothing
else. OCCT is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1,
with the Open CASCADE exception (the two texts are LICENSE_LGPL_21.txt and
OCCT_LGPL_EXCEPTION.txt, shipped in this folder). OpenChuckCAM itself is licensed
under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (LICENSE.txt).

Source offer. The complete corresponding source of the OCCT version used is
distributed in the Rust crate "occt-sys" 7.8.1 (https://crates.io/crates/occt-sys),
which is what the OpenChuckCAM build compiles. The source of OpenChuckCAM, including
the build scripts that link the kernel, is at the repository named in README.txt, at
the commit named there. Because the kernel is linked statically, you may rebuild
openchuck-step-worker against a modified OCCT: build it from that commit with
"cargo build --release -p openchuck-import --features kernel --bin openchuck-step-worker",
setting the environment variable OCCT_ROOT to your own OCCT installation.

The Open CASCADE exception text and the LGPL-2.1 text govern the kernel; where this
notice and those texts differ, they win.
